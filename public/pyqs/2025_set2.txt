Subtopic: Deadlock / Resource Allocation Graph
Difficulty: Hard
Marks: 2
Year: 2025
Question: In a system with single-instance resources R1, R2, R3, R4 and processes P1, P2, P3, P4, assignment edges are: R1→P1, R2→P2, R3→P3, R4→P4. Claim edges are: P1→R2, P2→R3, P3→R1, P2→R4, P4→R2. Which of the following makes the system deadlock-free?\nOption A: Aborting P1\nOption B: Aborting P3\nOption C: Aborting P2\nOption D: Aborting P1 and P4
Correct: A

Wait, aborting P1 alone leaves cycle P2→P4→P2. So C and D are the correct answers to completely clear deadlocks.

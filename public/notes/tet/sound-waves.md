Sound arises through vibration and ordinarily needs a medium. Airborne sound is a longitudinal pressure wave. Wavelength is distance per cycle, frequency cycles per second and period the time per cycle. Wave speed equals frequency times wavelength. Pitch broadly tracks frequency. Loudness depends on factors including amplitude and hearing sensitivity. Excessive noise can damage hearing and interfere with learning.

## Worked example

If sound frequency is 500 Hz and wavelength 0.68 m in air, the wave speed is 340 m/s.

## Common mistake

High pitch and loud sound describe different properties. A high-frequency sound can have small amplitude.

## Practice

Why can we see a lightning flash before hearing thunder?

**Answer:** Light travels through air much faster than sound.

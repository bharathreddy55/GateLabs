A quadrilateral's interior angles sum to 360°. A parallelogram has parallel opposite sides, a rectangle adds right angles, and a rhombus has equal sides. A square has both properties. For a convex polyhedron, vertices − edges + faces = 2. A net is a flat arrangement that folds into a solid. A line of symmetry reflects a figure onto itself. Rotational symmetry matches it after a turn smaller than 360°.

## Worked example

A cube has 8 vertices, 12 edges and 6 faces. Check 8−12+6=2. Draw a net with six squares and reason about which faces meet when it is folded.

## Common mistake

A solid's front view can hide depth and rear edges. Use other views or a model for a reliable count.

## Practice

What is the rotational symmetry order of a nonsquare rectangle?

**Answer:** Two, since it matches itself at a half turn and at a full turn.

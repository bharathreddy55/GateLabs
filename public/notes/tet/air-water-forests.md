Air supports respiration and weather, and nitrogen cycling moves nitrogen among the atmosphere, organisms and soils. Water moves through evaporation, condensation, precipitation, infiltration and runoff. Safe water depends on source protection, appropriate treatment and sanitation. Greenhouse gases affect climate, while some air pollutants contribute to acid deposition. Forests support habitats, carbon storage, livelihoods and water regulation. Endemic species occur naturally within a restricted area. Conservation includes protected areas and community action.

## Worked example

Compare a tank receiving sewage with a tank affected by drought. The first needs pollution control and safe supply measures, while the second also needs water allocation and storage planning.

## Common mistake

Clear water can still be contaminated. Appearance alone does not establish safety.

## Practice

Why does deforestation sometimes increase rapid runoff?

**Answer:** Loss of cover and disturbance can reduce infiltration and surface roughness, making more rainfall reach streams quickly.

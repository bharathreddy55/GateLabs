Speech refers to producing spoken sounds. Language is a broader symbolic system that includes comprehension, expression and social use. Development relies on children hearing and using meaningful language with responsive partners. Skinner emphasized reinforcement, Bandura observational learning, and Chomsky humans' capacity to acquire grammar. Each model emphasizes different processes. A multilingual child's knowledge can be distributed across languages and settings.

## Worked example

A teacher extends 'dog run' to 'the dog is running' in conversation, providing a meaningful model while acknowledging the child's message.

## Common mistake

An accent or a typical feature of second-language acquisition does not establish a disorder.

## Practice

How does a language-rich routine support development?

**Answer:** Repeated meaningful interaction gives children opportunities to hear, interpret and produce words and structures in context.

Cognition includes attention, perception, memory, reasoning, imagination, creativity and problem solving. Metacognition means planning and monitoring one's thinking. Piaget described changing patterns of reasoning, including concrete operations such as conservation. Vygotsky emphasized social mediation and the zone of proximal development, where guided assistance makes a harder task attainable. Bruner described enactive, iconic and symbolic representations and emphasized revisiting concepts at increasing levels of sophistication.

## Worked example

A child can repartition counters after the teacher models the first step. Reduce prompts as the child becomes independent. This uses scaffolding on a task in reach with help.

## Common mistake

Stage labels do not replace observing what a learner can do in a particular task and context.

Hardware includes input and output devices, while software provides instructions and applications. Digital resources include text, audio, video, simulations and presentations. Open educational resources carry permissions that allow specified kinds of reuse. Select a resource for its learning objective, accessibility, reliability and privacy. Use projectors, interactive displays or offline materials where available. Digital assessment helps only when it elicits useful evidence. Netiquette includes respectful communication and responsible sharing.

## Worked example

Use an offline simulation to let pupils vary the tilt of a ramp and record results. Provide captions or printed instructions and ask pupils to explain the relationship they observed.

## Common mistake

Free access alone does not establish permission to adapt or redistribute a digital resource. Check its licence.

## Practice

Why might a printed diagram be preferable to an animation in a lesson?

**Answer:** It can be easier to inspect at one's own pace and remains useful where connectivity or accessibility limits the animation.

Shelter reflects climate, available materials, costs, access and family needs. A family budget distinguishes recurring income and spending from occasional expenses. Local occupations involve specialized tools and services. Public institutions such as a primary health centre, panchayat, bank and post office serve different needs. Games and recreation involve movement, rules and participation, and teachers can adapt materials for access.

## Worked example

Ask learners to map a route from school to nearby community services. Compare why residents go to a primary health centre or a panchayat office.

## Common mistake

Study occupations without assigning ability or social value to people by gender, caste or family background.

## Practice

How can a teacher help pupils understand community dependence?

**Answer:** Trace how several people and services contribute to one familiar product or task.

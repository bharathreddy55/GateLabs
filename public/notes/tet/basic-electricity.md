Electric current is charge flow per unit time. Voltage is energy transferred per unit charge. Ohm's law gives V=IR for an ohmic conductor at fixed conditions. Series resistances add, while parallel branches share a voltage and their conductances add. Electric power is VI.

## Worked example

A 6 Ω resistor connected to a 12 V ideal source carries 2 A and dissipates 24 W.

## Common mistake

Series components carry the same current, while parallel components across the same nodes share voltage.

## Practice

Find the equivalent resistance of two 6 Ω resistors in parallel.

**Answer:** 3 Ω, because the reciprocal of the equivalent resistance is 1/6+1/6.

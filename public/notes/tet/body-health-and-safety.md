Sense organs respond to different stimuli. Bones support and protect the body, cartilage gives flexible support, joints permit movement and muscles pull across joints. Everyday health practices include hygiene, safe food and water, exercise and rest. Primary safety teaching covers trusted adults and personal boundaries, safe travel and water hazards. Teach school safety through clear reporting and dependable adult support. For a first-aid scenario prioritize scene safety and get trained help.

## Worked example

A pupil asks why the elbow bends while the middle of the forearm does not bend the same way. Point out the joints and model how muscles move bones there.

## Common mistake

Safety education should give children confidence to report inappropriate behaviour without placing responsibility for another person's actions on the child.

## Practice

How does a teacher include a visually impaired learner in a body-parts lesson?

**Answer:** Use accessible verbal descriptions and tactile models, and let the learner demonstrate the same conceptual goal.

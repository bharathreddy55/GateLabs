Study the French Revolution through citizenship and changing political authority, then compare nineteenth-century national unification in Germany and Italy. Global exchange began before industrialization, including Silk Road networks. Later steam, rail and communication accelerated movement under unequal colonial arrangements. The Great Depression sharply affected prices, trade and livelihoods. Industrialization transformed production, including Indian textiles, unevenly over time. Print lowered some barriers to circulation of debate, reform and education.

## Worked example

Falling global commodity prices can lower a grower's income even if output stays constant. Use this mechanism to connect a world economic shock with a local household.

## Common mistake

Political unification and cultural nationalism are related processes with different timings and institutions.

## Practice

How could print affect a social reform campaign?

**Answer:** Pamphlets and newspapers could circulate arguments, connect readers and sustain public debate across locations.

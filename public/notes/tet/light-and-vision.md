Transparent, translucent and opaque objects transmit light to different degrees. A shadow forms where an object blocks light. In reflection, incidence and reflection angles are measured from the normal. Smooth surfaces produce more regular reflection, while rough surfaces scatter light. Mirrors and lenses make images whose features depend on shape and object distance. A prism disperses white light, and the eye focuses light onto the retina. Braille encodes information through tactile dot patterns.

## Worked example

Move an opaque toy toward a point-like lamp while keeping the screen fixed. Its shadow generally grows as the toy approaches the source.

## Common mistake

The normal is perpendicular to the surface. Do not measure reflection angles from the mirror itself.

## Practice

Why can a pinhole camera image appear inverted?

**Answer:** Light from the top and bottom travels in straight lines through the hole and strikes opposite sides of the screen.

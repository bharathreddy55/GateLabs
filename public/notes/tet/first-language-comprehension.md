Read unfamiliar prose once for topic, audience and purpose, then reread for evidence. For poetry check speaker, image, repetition, mood and sound patterns. Distinguish a word's dictionary meaning from the meaning the surrounding lines give it. For prescribed texts revise genre, author, theme and the evidence for each interpretation in the applicable SCERT book.

## Worked example

If a poem mentions dry wells and waiting clouds repeatedly, the images support a theme of anticipation of rain. Quote the repeated image when justifying the inference.

## Common mistake

A poem's speaker and its author need not be the same person.

## Practice

What supports an inference about the mood of a verse?

**Answer:** Specific imagery, word choice and pattern in the verse.

Classical conditioning concerns learned associations between stimuli. Operant conditioning concerns how consequences change behaviour. Thorndike's law of effect links satisfying consequences with repetition. Insight learning involves reorganizing a problem. Observational learning uses attention to and retention of a model, reproduction and motivation. Constructivist teaching connects new evidence to prior ideas and gives learners opportunities to explain and revise them. The 5E approach uses engage, explore, explain, elaborate and evaluate.

## Worked example

A teacher praises pupils for showing checking steps and observes that checking increases. The praise functions as positive reinforcement because the target behaviour increases.

## Common mistake

Negative reinforcement increases behaviour through removal of an aversive condition. Punishment aims to decrease behaviour.

## Practice

A child imitates a peer's strategy after observing the peer solve a puzzle. Which theory best fits?

**Answer:** Observational learning, because the strategy is acquired from a model.

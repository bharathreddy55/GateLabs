Prepare unfamiliar poetry and prose and note the writer, subject and background of each prescribed piece. Study meanings, synonyms, different senses, opposites, similarly pronounced words and paired words. Methodology includes mother-tongue teaching, reading for knowledge, writing skills, activity-based lesson planning, materials and evaluation. Start with a text pupils can understand and ask them to support a new inference using evidence.

## Worked example

Take a paragraph about a village pond. Ask pupils to list details, infer one problem from them, and write a short solution. Mark reading evidence and written organization separately.

## Common mistake

A reading-fluency score gives limited evidence about what a learner inferred. Use comprehension questions as well.

## Practice

What would count as evidence that a child can infer meaning from context?

**Answer:** The child selects a plausible sense and explains which nearby wording supports it.

For ಸಮಾಸ expand the compound relation in a ವಿಗ್ರಹ ವಾಕ್ಯ. Revise ತತ್ಸಮ and ತದ್ಭವ through verified textbook pairs. ## Worked example

In ಅವನು ಸಿಂಹದಂತೆ ಹೋರಾಡಿದನು the word ಸಿಂಹದಂತೆ means 'like a lion'. The overt comparison is a clue for ಉಪಮೆ, or simile.

## Common mistake

Use a clause test to distinguish joined clauses from two words within one phrase.

## Practice

What is the role of a ವಿಗ್ರಹ ವಾಕ್ಯ in compound analysis?

**Answer:** It spells out how the two members are related in the meaning of the compound.

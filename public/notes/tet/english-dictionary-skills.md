For alphabetical order, compare letters from left to right through the first difference. A shorter identical prefix generally comes first. A dictionary entry may include pronunciation, part of speech, meanings, usage labels and examples. Select the sense that fits the sentence. Ordinal numbers show position and include first, second, third and fourth.

## Worked example

Order 'play', 'player' and 'plea' alphabetically. 'Play' is a prefix of 'player', and the third letter a precedes e in 'plea', so the order is play, player, plea.

## Common mistake

The first sense listed may not fit the context. Compare the part of speech and nearby words.

## Practice

Which dictionary sense of 'bank' fits 'They sat on the bank of the river'?

**Answer:** The land at the side of a river, because the surrounding phrase specifies the sense.

Casteism describes exclusion, prejudice or preferential treatment organized around caste. It can operate through overt practices and through institutional rules applied in ways that systematically disadvantage groups. Article 17 abolishes untouchability, and the Protection of Civil Rights Act addresses its practice. The SC/ST Prevention of Atrocities Act covers specified offences and protections under its terms.

## Worked example

A public facility blocks access on grounds of untouchability. Article 17 is the most directly relevant constitutional provision for that clue.

## Common mistake

Use the facts to identify the conduct. Caste identity, reservation policy and caste discrimination describe different things.

## Practice

How can hiring through a closed social network sustain exclusion?

**Answer:** Applicants outside the network may never receive information or referrals despite having the necessary qualifications.

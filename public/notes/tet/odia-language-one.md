Revise Odia prose, poetry, literary forms and word meaning in their textual context. Practise ନାମପଦ, ସର୍ବନାମ, କ୍ରିୟାପଦ, gender, number, tense, case, sandhi, samasa and conventional expressions from the official reader. Read slowly enough to distinguish related letter forms and conjuncts.

## Worked example

In ସେ ବହି ପଢ଼ୁଛି, ସେ is the pronoun, ବହି refers to the book and ପଢ଼ୁଛି expresses reading in progress.

## Common mistake

Use the sentence context to resolve a pronoun or a word with multiple senses.

## Practice

Which word in ସେ ବହି ପଢ଼ୁଛି identifies the action?

**Answer:** ପଢ଼ୁଛି, the verb phrase meaning 'is reading'.

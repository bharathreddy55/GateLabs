Autotrophs make organic matter from inorganic inputs, while heterotrophs obtain nutrients from organisms or their products. Human digestion breaks food into absorbable molecules. Amoebae can engulf food and digest it in vacuoles. Cellular respiration releases usable energy. Blood distributes substances in animals. Xylem chiefly carries water and minerals, and phloem distributes sugars in plants. Excretion removes metabolic wastes. Nerves transmit signals and support coordination.

## Worked example

Compare breathing, which moves air in and out, with cellular respiration, which releases usable energy in cells. Breathing supports respiration by exchanging gases.

## Common mistake

Excretion removes metabolic waste, while egestion removes undigested material. Distinguish them in a process question.

## Practice

Which plant tissue mainly distributes sugars made in leaves?

**Answer:** Phloem, transporting organic solutes between sources and sinks.

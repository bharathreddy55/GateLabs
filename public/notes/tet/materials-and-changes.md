Classify materials by properties such as solubility, hardness, conductivity and reaction. Filtration separates insoluble particles from fluid. Evaporation can concentrate a solution, and crystallization separates a dissolved solid as crystals under suitable conditions. Indicators respond to acidity or basicity. Acid–base neutralization changes chemical composition. Rusting forms new substances when iron reacts under suitable conditions, and galvanization coats iron or steel with zinc.

## Worked example

Separate sand and dissolved salt by filtering the sand from the liquid, then collecting salt from the filtrate by evaporation or crystallization.

## Common mistake

A clear solution can still contain dissolved substances. Passing it through ordinary filter paper will not separate those dissolved particles.

## Practice

Is melting ice generally a chemical change?

**Answer:** No. The substance remains water, with a change of physical phase.

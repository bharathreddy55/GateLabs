Learners differ in background knowledge, interests, experience, rate of practice and performance profiles. Spearman proposed a general factor and task-specific factors. Thurstone described primary mental abilities, and Guilford described dimensions of intellect. Gardner proposed multiple intelligences as a broad framework. An IQ score summarizes performance on specified standardized tasks and should be interpreted with its measurement and cultural limitations.

## Worked example

When a pupil explains a concept orally but writes slowly, offer appropriate response formats while identifying the knowledge and writing skills separately.

## Common mistake

Multiple representation and varied practice can help many learners. Assigning each child to one fixed learning style is an unsupported inference.

## Practice

What is Spearman's g?

**Answer:** A proposed general factor shared across performances on different mental tasks.

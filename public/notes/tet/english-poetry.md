Identify the speaker, situation and recurring images before inferring tone or meaning. Simile compares with like or as, metaphor presents one thing through another, and personification gives human qualities to something nonhuman. Rhyme and repetition can emphasize an idea or create expectation. Support each interpretation with a word or line from the poem.

## Worked example

In 'The tired street yawned at dawn', the human action given to the street is personification. It can suggest a quiet place slowly becoming active.

## Common mistake

A poetic image can support more than one interpretation. Select the option best supported by the complete passage.

## Practice

What figure appears in 'The stars were scattered diamonds'?

**Answer:** Metaphor, because the stars are presented directly as diamonds without like or as.

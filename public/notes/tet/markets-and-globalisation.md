Goods move between producers and consumers through wholesale, retail and digital markets. Money provides a common payment and accounting unit. Credit gives present purchasing power against future repayment and may involve interest, security and risk. Imports and exports link economies through trade, technology and supply chains. Consumer rights include access to appropriate information and routes for redress under the applicable framework. Development includes public facilities such as water, transport and health.

## Worked example

Compare a small loan with weekly repayments and a bank loan repaid monthly. Convert costs to the same time basis and check fees and conditions before comparing.

## Common mistake

A low stated interest amount can omit fees. Compare total cost and the timing of payments.

## Practice

Why might several buyers improve a farmer's bargaining position?

**Answer:** The farmer can compare offers and is less dependent on the terms set by one buyer.

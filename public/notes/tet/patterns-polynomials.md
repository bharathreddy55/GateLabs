Describe a pattern in words, a table, an expression or a graph. Distinguish terms, factors and coefficients. Distributivity supports multiplication, expansion and factoring. For nonzero x, division of powers subtracts exponents. A linear graph y=mx+c changes by m units in y for each unit in x and crosses the y-axis at c. Polynomial division proceeds by cancelling successive leading terms.

## Worked example

A pattern adds three tiles with each new stage and starts at five tiles in stage 1. The nth stage has 5+3(n−1)=3n+2 tiles. Plotting stage and tile count produces points on a line.

## Common mistake

Read the starting index before assigning an intercept. Stage 1 has five tiles in the example, even though the line's intercept is two.

## Practice

Expand (x+3)(x−3).

**Answer:** x²−9, the difference of squares identity.

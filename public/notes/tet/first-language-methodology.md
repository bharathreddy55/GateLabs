Teach listening, speaking, reading and writing as connected skills. Start from meaningful language the learners recognize, model a target feature, provide guided use, and give feedback on an independent sample. Use stories, discussion, charts and local materials. Support multilingual learners with bridging activities and assess the particular skill rather than relying on one overall fluency impression.

## Worked example

For a new narrative genre, read a short story aloud, retell it with children, mark sequence words together and ask each learner to write a different ending.

## Common mistake

Grammar correction is most useful when it responds to the learning goal and preserves a reason to communicate.

## Practice

How can a teacher assess understanding of oral instructions?

**Answer:** Give a new instruction with the same structure and observe whether the learner can carry it out or explain it.

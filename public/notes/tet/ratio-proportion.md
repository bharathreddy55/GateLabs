A ratio compares quantities multiplicatively. Dividing a total in ratio a:b gives shares a/(a+b) and b/(a+b). In direct proportion y/x stays constant. In inverse proportion xy stays constant under the problem's assumptions.

## Worked example

Split ₹1,250 in ratio 2:3. There are five parts worth ₹250 each. The shares are ₹500 and ₹750.

## Common mistake

Convert mixed units before forming a ratio. A part-to-part ratio differs from a part-to-whole fraction.

## Practice

Six identical taps fill a tank in 10 hours. Four work at the same individual rate. How long do they take?

**Answer:** 15 hours, with total work equal to 60 tap-hours and four taps operating.

Plants and animals supply foods with varied nutrients. Carbohydrates and fats provide energy, proteins supply amino acids, and vitamins and minerals support many functions. Balanced diets meet energy and nutrient needs and can take different cultural forms. Compare cooking, drying, salting and refrigeration by the conditions they change. School meals can support learning and nutrition. Agricultural practices include preparing soil, planting, watering, protecting crops, harvesting and storing them.

## Worked example

Beans and grains can complement one another in a varied meal. Include vegetables and other foods to supply micronutrients and fibre.

## Common mistake

A food is not adequately described by one nutrient. Several nutrients are usually present in different amounts.

## Practice

Which condition does drying change in food preservation?

**Answer:** It reduces available water, which can limit growth of many microbes.

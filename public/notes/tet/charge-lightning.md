There are two signs of electric charge. Like charges repel and unlike charges attract. Rubbing can transfer electrons between materials, leaving opposite charges. A charged material can attract a neutral object through redistribution of charge. Lightning is a large electrical discharge caused by charge separation. Follow official safety instructions and move to appropriate shelter during a thunderstorm.

## Worked example

A charged comb can attract neutral paper because charges shift slightly in the paper. The nearby side experiences stronger attraction than the farther side experiences repulsion.

## Common mistake

An attractive force does not prove both objects have opposite net charges. One can be neutral and polarized.

## Practice

What is the purpose of a properly installed lightning protection system?

**Answer:** It provides a designed conductive path that helps carry lightning current toward earth.

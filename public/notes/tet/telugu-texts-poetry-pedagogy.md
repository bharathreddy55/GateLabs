Use the prescribed readers for authors, genres, aims and themes, including differences between పద్యం and గద్యం. Build examples of synonyms, నానార్థాలు, వ్యుత్పత్తి, idioms, proverbs, riddles and dialect words. For ఛందస్సు identify గురు and లఘు syllables and compare the prescribed patterns of ఉత్పలమాల and చంపకమాల. For అలంకారాలు name the feature and quote the supporting wording. In mother-tongue teaching align the lesson goal, reading or listening material, guided practice and formative feedback.

## Worked example

In 'ఆమె ముఖం చంద్రునిలా ఉంది', చంద్రునిలా makes an explicit comparison. A teacher can ask pupils to replace the image while retaining the comparison structure.

## Common mistake

A figure of speech and a metrical pattern answer different questions. Rhythm concerns form, while the comparison concerns language use.

## Practice

How can a teacher check a proverb's meaning?

**Answer:** Ask learners to match it to a plausible situation and explain the connection rather than repeat the words.

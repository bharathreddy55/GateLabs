Resources are distributed unevenly because geological history controls minerals, while climate, landforms and drainage control water, soils and forests. India's coalfields are concentrated in several Gondwana basins, including the Damodar valley. Iron ore occurs in several mineral belts and is linked to steel production through transport networks. In Andhra Pradesh, the Kadapa basin is known for minerals including barytes, and limestone deposits support cement production. Sun and wind are flows whose usefulness depends on location, grids and seasonal variation.

## Worked example

A cement producer choosing a bulky raw material close to its plant prioritizes limestone deposits and reliable transport. A coastal oil or gas facility also needs suitable handling and pipeline links.

## Common mistake

Distinguish a geological occurrence from a reserve that can be economically extracted under current conditions.

## Practice

Why is mineral distribution uneven?

**Answer:** Different rock types, depositional environments and geological processes concentrate different minerals.

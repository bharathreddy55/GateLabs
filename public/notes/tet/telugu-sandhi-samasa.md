సంధి examines the sound change where forms join. Practise joining and separating examples of native rules such as అత్వ, ఇత్వ, ఉత్వ and యడాగమ and Sanskrit patterns such as సవర్ణదీర్ఘ, గుణ, యణ and వృద్ధి. సమాసం concerns how two or more words form a compound with a specific semantic relation. A విగ్రహవాక్యం expands that relation into a phrase. Study whether the meaning depends on one member, both members or an external referent.

## Worked example

రామ + ఆలయం becomes రామాలయం through coalescence into a long vowel. Reading it as రాముని ఆలయం instead expands the compound meaning, the temple of Rama. A single word can invite both sound and meaning analysis.

## Common mistake

A split for సంధి identifies input sounds or words. A విగ్రహవాక్యం explains the word relationship in a fuller phrase.

## Practice

What does a విగ్రహవాక్యం show?

**Answer:** It makes the semantic and grammatical relationship inside a compound explicit.

Use Urdu speech, listening, reading and writing together. Begin with a meaningful oral example, then model how familiar words appear in print and ask learners to use them independently. Write lesson objectives as observable skills, pick supports such as word cards and stories, and distinguish ongoing formative assessment from an endpoint evaluation. The syllabus also asks about institutions supporting Urdu. Illustrative national institutions include the National Council for Promotion of Urdu Language.

## Worked example

Provide word cards for گھر and کتاب, ask pupils to read and sort the cards, then use either word in a new oral sentence. Use their answers to see whether print recognition transfers to meaning.

## Common mistake

Fluent recitation does not guarantee comprehension. Ask an unfamiliar question about a familiar reading.

## Practice

What can an assessment during a reading lesson reveal?

**Answer:** It can show which letters, words or ideas need more modelling before the class moves on.

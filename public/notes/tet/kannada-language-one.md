## Worked example

In ಮಗು ಪುಸ್ತಕ ಓದುತ್ತಿದೆ, ಮಗು is the subject and ಓದುತ್ತಿದೆ (is reading) is the verb form. Compare its present meaning with the completed ಓದಿತು.

## Common mistake

In a grammar item, identify the base and suffix separately before assigning number or case.

## Practice

In ಅವಳು ಓದುತ್ತಾಳೆ, which word is a pronoun?

**Answer:** ಅವಳು, because it stands for a person rather than naming her.

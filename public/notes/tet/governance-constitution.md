Distinguish monarchy and democracy, then map Indian governance across Union, state and local institutions. Legislatures make laws and scrutinize government. Executives carry out policy, and courts adjudicate disputes under the law. The Constitution sets out rights, duties and the distribution of powers. Federalism shares public authority across levels. Power sharing can also address conflict through institutional accommodation, as in comparisons of Belgium and Sri Lanka. Parties organize competition, and democracy is evaluated through participation, accountability and outcomes.

## Worked example

A village road drainage complaint can start with local authorities. A constitutional rights dispute may eventually come before a court. The two institutions have different functions.

## Common mistake

A majority in a legislature operates under constitutional rules. It does not erase individual rights or institutional checks.

## Practice

What makes an independent judiciary important?

**Answer:** It can decide disputes and review government action without being directed by a party to the dispute.

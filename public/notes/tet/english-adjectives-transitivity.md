Adjectives used together tend to follow a conventional sequence from opinion to size, age, shape, colour, origin, material and purpose, with context affecting order. A transitive use of a verb takes a direct object. Many verbs allow both transitive and intransitive uses with different structures.

## Worked example

'A small red wooden box' orders size, colour and material. In 'The child opened the door', door is a direct object. In 'The door opened', opened has an intransitive use.

## Common mistake

Classify the verb in its sentence. A verb that takes an object in one use can occur without a direct object in another.

## Practice

Is 'slept' transitive in 'The baby slept peacefully'?

**Answer:** No. 'Peacefully' is an adverb and there is no direct object.

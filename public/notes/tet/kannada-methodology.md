Prepare unfamiliar prose and verse as well as the authors, poems and background of prescribed and supplementary lessons. Learn meaning, synonyms, multiple senses, idioms and proverbs through complete utterances. Set objectives for the mother-tongue skills, choose resources, plan instruction, and gather both formative and summative evidence. Use a lesson text to teach a specific skill while maintaining the text's meaning.

## Worked example

Show pupils two uses of a word from their readers. Have pairs explain the meaning in each sentence and then write a new example of one use.

## Common mistake

A word can have multiple senses. Use the wider sentence to establish what was meant in a lesson.

## Practice

How can a teacher check whether a learner understands a Kannada idiom?

**Answer:** Give a new everyday situation and ask which expression fits and why.

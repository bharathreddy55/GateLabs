Cells are the basic structural and functional units of living organisms. Cell membranes regulate exchange, and genetic material carries inherited information. Typical plant cells have cell walls and large vacuoles, and photosynthetic cells usually have chloroplasts. Microorganisms include bacteria, microscopic fungi and protozoa. Some support decomposition or food production, and others cause disease. Viruses replicate using host cells.

## Worked example

Milk fermentation involves microorganisms changing substances in milk. Use this to show that microbes can be beneficial in one context and harmful in another.

## Common mistake

Not every cell in a plant contains chloroplasts. Roots often lack chloroplasts because they do not usually receive light.

## Practice

What is one difference between a typical plant cell and a typical animal cell?

**Answer:** The plant cell has a cell wall outside its membrane.

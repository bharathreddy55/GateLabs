Inclusive primary classrooms provide access, participation and progress for learners with diverse needs. Early identification uses observations, assessment and specialist input where appropriate. An individualized education plan sets goals, supports and a way to review progress. Differentiation adjusts materials, support or response while preserving a meaningful learning target. Use multiple representations, accessible materials and flexible groupings.

## Worked example

A learner with decoding difficulty can listen to the same story to show comprehension while receiving targeted decoding instruction.

## Common mistake

Access supports and individual instruction can occur together. Do not assume the accommodation removes the need to teach the relevant skill.

## Practice

What makes an IEP goal measurable?

**Answer:** It specifies the skill, conditions, success criterion and time frame so progress can be checked.

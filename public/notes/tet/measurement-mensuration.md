Use standard units and distinguish length, mass, capacity, time, area and volume. Perimeter measures a boundary and area counts enclosed square units. A trapezium's area is half the sum of parallel sides times height. A cuboid's volume is length × breadth × height and total surface area is 2(lb+bh+hl). Use a calendar or timeline for elapsed days, and align currency units in money problems.

## Worked example

If a lesson starts at 10:45 and ends at 12:10, count 15 minutes to 11:00 and 70 more to 12:10, for 85 minutes.

## Common mistake

A conversion of 100 cm per metre becomes 10,000 cm² per m². Distinguish conversion of length from area.

## Practice

What is the perimeter of a 5 cm by 3 cm rectangle?

**Answer:** 16 cm, or 2(5+3).

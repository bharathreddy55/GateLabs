Force can change motion or shape. Add forces as vectors to find the net force. Gravity is a noncontact force, while contact forces include normal reaction and friction. Pressure is normal force per area. Fluid pressure tends to rise with depth in a resting fluid. Friction resists relative sliding or its tendency and can provide grip, while lubrication can reduce it.

## Worked example

Use the same downward force of 100 N on 0.02 m² and 0.01 m². The pressures are 5,000 Pa and 10,000 Pa respectively. The smaller contact area produces higher pressure.

## Common mistake

A force and a pressure have different units. Compare contact areas before deciding which pressure is greater.

## Practice

Why can broad straps feel more comfortable with the same schoolbag?

**Answer:** They spread the supporting force over a larger area and reduce pressure.

Tense locates events in time and aspect presents their course or relation to another time. Progressive uses a form of be plus -ing. Perfect uses have plus a past participle. In a transitive passive, the active object becomes subject, and a form of be plus the past participle preserves the tense and aspect as appropriate.

## Worked example

'The children planted a tree' becomes 'A tree was planted by the children'. The past tense is carried by was.

## Common mistake

Do not change the event time when changing voice. In a passive, the agent phrase can be omitted if it is unknown or irrelevant.

## Practice

Which choice describes an ongoing action at a past time, 'I read' or 'I was reading'?

**Answer:** I was reading, the past progressive.

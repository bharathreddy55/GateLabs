Classify words by their function in context. Nouns name, verbs express an action or state, adjectives describe nouns and adverbs can modify verbs, adjectives or other adverbs. Use a or an with an indefinite singular count noun according to the following sound. The normally marks a specific identifiable referent. Find the head subject to establish agreement.

## Worked example

'An hour' uses an because hour begins with a vowel sound. In 'The list of names is long', list is the head subject, so the verb is is.

## Common mistake

A word can change function. 'Fast' is an adjective in 'a fast train' and an adverb in 'runs fast'.

## Practice

Choose: The box of books is/are heavy.

**Answer:** Is, because box is singular and of books is a dependent phrase.

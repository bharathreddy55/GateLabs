Review prose, verse, authors and genres from the applicable reader. In vocabulary distinguish पर्यायवाची, विलोम, अनेकार्थी, मुहावरे and लोकोक्तियाँ. Practise संज्ञा, सर्वनाम, विशेषण, क्रिया, लिंग, वचन, काल, संधि, समास, वाक्य-रचना and punctuation using sentences rather than isolated labels.

## Worked example

In बच्चे मैदान में खेल रहे हैं, बच्चे and हैं signal plural agreement, and खेल रहे हैं describes an ongoing activity.

## Common mistake

A मुहावरा has a conventional meaning in use. Translating its individual words literally can miss the intended meaning.

## Practice

In सीता ने मीठा फल खाया, what is मीठा?

**Answer:** विशेषण, because it describes फल.

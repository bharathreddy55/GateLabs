Children learn social roles through family, peers, teachers, media and cultural routines. Cooperation can teach turn-taking and conflict resolution. Explicit rules and predictable consequences support classroom safety. Emotional development includes recognizing feelings, describing them and using regulation strategies. An adult can model naming a feeling, pausing and considering a constructive action. Bullying requires attention to repetition, power imbalance and safety.

## Worked example

A group repeatedly excludes one child and threatens peers who intervene. The teacher stops the behaviour, checks safety, records the pattern, and follows up with structured support and accountability.

## Common mistake

Treat a single disagreement and sustained bullying as different situations that may require different responses.

## Practice

What classroom strategy supports emotional regulation?

**Answer:** Model how to notice frustration, label it, pause and choose a problem-solving response.

Area measures a surface and uses square units. Volume measures space and uses cubic units. Common formulas include triangle area ½bh, circle area πr², cylinder volume πr²h and cone volume ⅓πr²h. Similar figures scaled by k have k² times the area and k³ times the volume.

## Worked example

A cylinder of radius 3 cm and height 10 cm has volume 90π cm³. Its curved surface area is 60π cm².

## Common mistake

A diameter must be halved before substituting a radius formula.

## Practice

An open-top tank has a 2 m by 3 m base and 1.5 m of water. Find the volume.

**Answer:** 9 m³, the base area 6 m² multiplied by depth.

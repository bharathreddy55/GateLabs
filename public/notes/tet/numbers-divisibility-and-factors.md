Prime numbers have exactly two positive divisors. Factor a positive integer into primes to find all divisors or to combine integers. The GCD uses the smallest exponents across factorizations, and the LCM uses the largest. The divisibility test for 2 uses the last digit, for 3 or 9 the digit sum, and for 4 the last two digits.

## Worked example

For 36=2²×3² and 48=2⁴×3, the GCD is 2²×3=12 and the LCM is 2⁴×3²=144.

## Common mistake

One has exactly one positive divisor and is neither prime nor composite.

## Practice

How can you test 2,340 for divisibility by 9?

**Answer:** Sum the digits to get 9. Since 9 divides that sum, it divides 2,340.

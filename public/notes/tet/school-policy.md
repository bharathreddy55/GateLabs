The Right to Education Act implements the right to free and compulsory education for children aged six to fourteen within its terms. The RTI Act concerns access to information held by public authorities subject to its provisions. National curriculum frameworks guide curricular thinking. NEP 2020 articulates education policy, and NCF for School Education 2023 translates curricular directions into stage-based expectations and guidance. The syllabus also refers to earlier frameworks and Andhra Pradesh curriculum documents.

## Worked example

A question about a child's entitlement to elementary schooling concerns RTE. A request for a public authority's published school allocation concerns access to information under RTI.

## Common mistake

A policy, curriculum framework and enacted law differ in legal force and function.

## Practice

What is the relationship between Article 21A and RTE?

**Answer:** Article 21A provides the constitutional right, and the RTE Act gives that right a statutory framework.

Archaeological evidence such as tools, rock art, seeds and animal remains helps explain changing livelihoods. Settled agriculture expanded in some regions alongside continued mobile livelihoods. Mahajanapadas included monarchies and republic-like polities. Magadha became a centre of imperial power. For the Deccan and south, study Satavahana, Pallava and Chalukya rule through inscriptions, trade, art and temples. Later Kakatiya and Vijayanagara examples illustrate different regional states and patterns of irrigation and patronage.

## Worked example

A record of an irrigation tank and temple grant can reveal agricultural investment and patronage. Compare it with a royal eulogy before drawing broad conclusions about daily life.

## Common mistake

Different kinds of evidence answer different questions. A sculpture or coin does not establish every claim in a later chronicle.

## Practice

What is a reason to compare excavated remains with inscriptions?

**Answer:** Each shows different aspects of society, and comparing them can confirm or complicate an interpretation.

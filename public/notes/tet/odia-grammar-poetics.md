Study sound and writing, independent letters and vowel marks, conjunct forms and word categories. In ପଦ analysis identify noun, adjective, pronoun, verb and indeclinable words by their function. ସନ୍ଧି concerns joining and sound change. ସମାସ analysis expands the combined word to show its relation. ବାକ୍ୟ can be classified by structure and communicative use. For ଛନ୍ଦ and ଅଳଙ୍କାର, compare definitions, types and concrete textbook examples.

## Worked example

In 'ସେ ଲାଲ ଫୁଲ ଦେଖିଲା', ଲାଲ describes ଫୁଲ. That function is adjectival. The sentence gives a practical way to identify a word class.

## Common mistake

Use a textual example to justify a literary figure. A word shared by two lines is not automatically a figure of speech.

## Practice

What helps distinguish a synonym from a homophone in an Odia exercise?

**Answer:** A synonym is about shared meaning, whereas a homophone is about shared pronunciation.

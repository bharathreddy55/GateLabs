Read Urdu prose and poetry in their script. Build contextual vocabulary including مترادف, متضاد, محاورہ and ضرب المثل. Review اسم, فعل and حرف, gender and number, sentence structure, spelling and punctuation. Match a prescribed author and genre to the official reader, and practise oral-to-written language activities.

## Worked example

In بچہ کتاب پڑھتا ہے, بچہ is a noun and پڑھتا ہے is the verb phrase. With بچی the corresponding form is پڑھتی ہے.

## Common mistake

Use the word's function and agreement in the sentence. Similar-looking stems can take different endings.

## Practice

In the pair خوش and اداس, what is the lexical relationship?

**Answer:** They are antonyms in the ordinary sense of happy and sad.

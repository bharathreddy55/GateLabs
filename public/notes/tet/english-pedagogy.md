Use meaningful tasks at an appropriate difficulty. Model pronunciation, vocabulary or structure, then move from supported practice to genuine use. Before reading, activate relevant knowledge. During reading, guide attention to evidence. After reading, use discussion, retelling or writing. Diagnose errors by skill and give specific feedback. Plan objectives, resources and assessment together.

## Worked example

For a lesson on making polite requests, learners listen to a model, practise variations in pairs and then ask classmates for materials in a task with a real purpose.

## Common mistake

Repeating an isolated form demonstrates recall. An authentic conversation can reveal appropriate use and comprehension.

## Practice

How can a teacher assess listening comprehension without confounding it with spelling?

**Answer:** Let learners select a picture, sequence actions or respond orally to demonstrate what they understood.

Represent a rational number as a/b with integer a and nonzero b. Equal fractions arise by multiplying numerator and denominator by the same nonzero number. Add fractions using common denominators. Multiply numerators and denominators directly. To divide by a nonzero fraction, multiply by its reciprocal. Number lines help compare negative rational numbers and give examples between two numbers.

## Worked example

To add 3/4 and 2/3, rewrite them as 9/12 and 8/12. The sum is 17/12. The denominator indicates the size of the common unit.

## Common mistake

Adding denominators changes the unit. Use a model to show why 1/2 + 1/2 makes one whole.

## Practice

Give a rational number strictly between 1/4 and 1/2.

**Answer:** 3/8. Rewrite the bounds as 2/8 and 4/8 and choose 3/8.

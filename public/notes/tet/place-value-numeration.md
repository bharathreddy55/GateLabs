Face value is the digit itself. Place value multiplies that digit by the value of its position. Indian grouping places a comma after three digits from the right and then at intervals of two. International grouping commonly uses groups of three. Both group the same base-ten number and use different names for some positions.

## Worked example

12,34,567 in the Indian system is 1,234,567 in the international system. The digit 3 represents 30,000 in either notation.

## Common mistake

A comma changes grouping for reading and does not change the number's value.

## Practice

What is the place value of 7 in 7,05,420?

**Answer:** Seven lakh, or 700,000.

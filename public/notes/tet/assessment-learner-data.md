Diagnostic assessment locates prior understanding and errors. Formative assessment guides next instruction during learning. Summative assessment describes achievement at an endpoint. Use observations, interviews, tasks, rubrics and quizzes chosen for the skill. Maintain clear criteria and constructive feedback. Mean, median, frequency and charts illuminate different features of learner data. Continuous and comprehensive evaluation emphasizes ongoing evidence across a range of outcomes.

## Worked example

If many pupils write 1/3 + 1/4 = 2/7, collect their reasoning and teach common units with fraction models. Reassess with a new example.

## Common mistake

A tool is formative when its evidence guides learning. A short quiz can be formative or summative depending on its use.

## Practice

Why might median be preferable when one score is unusually low?

**Answer:** Median depends on rank, so it is less sensitive than mean to an isolated extreme.

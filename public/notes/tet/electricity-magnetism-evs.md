A circuit needs a conducting path and a source. A switch opens or closes that path. A fuse breaks a circuit under excessive current, and an MCB can interrupt the circuit through its protection mechanism. Current can produce heat, magnetic fields and chemical effects. Electroplating uses electric current to deposit a material. A compass magnet aligns with the local magnetic field. An electromagnet strengthens a magnetic field using a current-carrying coil, often around an iron core.

## Worked example

Place a compass near a current-carrying wire and observe deflection. Changing current direction changes the magnetic effect, illustrating how an electromagnet can be controlled.

## Common mistake

All electrical demonstrations should use suitable classroom low-voltage equipment. Never connect a simple learning circuit to mains.

## Practice

Why does a fuse need an appropriate rating?

**Answer:** It must carry normal operating current and open the circuit when excessive current presents a hazard.

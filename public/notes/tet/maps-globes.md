A globe models Earth's shape, while a map represents a selected area on a flat surface. Latitude measures angular position north or south of the equator, and longitude east or west of a reference meridian. A legend explains symbols, an orientation mark indicates direction, and scale relates map distance to ground distance. Local maps can show links among settlements, mandals and districts.

## Worked example

At scale 1:50,000, 2 cm on the map represents 100,000 cm or 1 km on the ground.

## Common mistake

A map's top points north only if the orientation supports that assumption. Check the arrow or grid.

## Practice

Which map is better for seeing streets in one village, 1:5,000 or 1:500,000?

**Answer:** 1:5,000, because that larger-scale map shows a smaller area in greater detail.

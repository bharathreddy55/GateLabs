Statements, questions, commands and exclamations serve different purposes. In reported speech, adjust pronouns and time or place references when the reporting context changes. A reported yes-no question usually takes if or whether and statement word order. A reported command often uses an infinitive with an appropriate reporting verb.

## Worked example

She asked, 'Are you ready?' becomes 'She asked whether I was ready' when the reporter was the person addressed.

## Common mistake

Avoid carrying question inversion into indirect questions. Use 'where the book was', rather than 'where was the book'.

## Practice

Report 'Close the door', the teacher said to Ravi.

**Answer:** The teacher told Ravi to close the door.

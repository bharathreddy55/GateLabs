Plan lessons around familiar situations with observable evidence. Science methods can use observation, prediction, experiments and classification. Social studies methods can use maps, timelines, discussion, role-play and source comparison. Ask learners to record reasons, not just results. Offer models, local materials, field observations and accessible alternatives. Use feedback and reassessment to diagnose gaps in understanding.

## Worked example

Ask a class to compare shade and sun temperatures at fixed times, record data, map the schoolyard, and discuss where to place shade trees. This combines measurement, geography and practical reasoning.

## Common mistake

A field activity needs a clear learning goal and a way for learners to demonstrate what they discovered.

## Practice

What assessment can show whether children understand a local-water map?

**Answer:** Ask them to trace a route or infer where runoff goes and explain the evidence from the map.

India's major physical regions include the Himalayan mountains, the northern river plains, the Peninsular Plateau, the Indian desert, the coastal plains and the islands. Himalayan rivers and their tributaries laid down much of the alluvium of the northern plains. The Peninsular Plateau contains ancient crystalline and sedimentary terrains, with the Western and Eastern Ghats defining parts of its margins.

In Andhra Pradesh, locate the Bay of Bengal coastal plain, the discontinuous Eastern Ghats and the inland plateau. The Krishna and Godavari build broad lowland deltas. The Nallamala ranges and the Kadapa basin help distinguish the southern interior, while the Araku region illustrates higher terrain in the northern Eastern Ghats. River valleys interrupt the Ghats and link upland catchments to coastal plains.

## Worked example

Identify the region most likely to contain broad, flat tracts built by river sediment. Choose the Krishna or Godavari delta. Hill valleys in the Eastern Ghats may contain local alluvium but have very different relief.

## Common mistake

Distinguish a landform from an administrative region. Coastal Andhra includes hills and river valleys as well as the coastal plain.

## Practice

Why are the Eastern Ghats described as discontinuous?

**Answer:** Major rivers cut through the hill systems, and the hills occur as separate ranges rather than a continuous escarpment.

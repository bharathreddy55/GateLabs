Match the text's purpose and audience to its form. A formal letter has an address or recipient, greeting, subject where appropriate, body and sign-off. Email typically uses a clear subject and appropriate opening. Diary entries record dated experiences in a personal voice. Notices and invitations specify event, place, time and required action. Paragraphs develop one controlling idea. Dialogues use identifiable turns, biographies use selected life events, and speeches address listeners directly.

## Worked example

A school notice should say what is happening, who should attend, where and when it occurs, and any deadline or materials needed. Give the details in a form a pupil can scan quickly.

## Common mistake

A diary and a notice can describe the same event. Choose the perspective and format the prompt asks for.

## Practice

Which details are essential in an invitation to a reading session?

**Answer:** Purpose, date, time, location and intended invitees. Add response instructions if needed.

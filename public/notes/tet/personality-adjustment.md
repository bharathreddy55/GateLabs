Personality concerns relatively enduring patterns of thought, feeling and behaviour, influenced by temperament, relationships, culture and experience. Trait theories describe stable dimensions, psychodynamic approaches emphasize unconscious conflict, and social-cognitive approaches examine interactions among person, behaviour and environment. Inventories use structured responses. Projective approaches invite interpretations of ambiguous material and have measurement limitations. Guidance supports choices and information, while counselling involves a more focused helping process. Signs of distress call for support and appropriate referral.

## Worked example

A pupil with repeated worry about tests avoids class. Talk privately, identify triggers, adjust immediate demands where appropriate and involve school support, rather than assigning a personality label.

## Common mistake

A teacher's observation can motivate support. It cannot by itself establish a clinical diagnosis.

## Practice

What distinguishes a structured questionnaire from a projective task?

**Answer:** A questionnaire asks standardized items. A projective task asks for responses to ambiguous material.

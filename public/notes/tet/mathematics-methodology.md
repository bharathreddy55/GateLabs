Set an objective that combines a concept with a visible action. Move between objects, diagrams, words and symbols. Ask learners to explain and compare methods. A diagnostic response reveals the error pattern, a remedial activity addresses that pattern, and reassessment checks transfer. Select textbooks and teaching materials for the objective rather than following an activity mechanically.

## Worked example

A child reads 307 as 37. Use hundreds, tens and ones materials to show the empty tens place, then ask the child to represent 405 without a model.

## Common mistake

Speed and accuracy provide different information. A learner can compute fast while misunderstanding the operation.

## Practice

What feedback helps a child who says a longer rectangle must have larger area?

**Answer:** Invite the child to count square units in counterexamples so the comparison depends on both dimensions.

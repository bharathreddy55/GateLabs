Revise स्वर, व्यंजन, वर्ण-विच्छेद, संयुक्ताक्षर and doubled consonants. Distinguish विकारी forms such as nouns and verbs whose forms can change from अविकारी forms such as many conjunctions. Practise कारक, gender, number, tense and punctuation. उपसर्ग comes before a base and प्रत्यय follows it. Compare सकर्मक movement of action onto an object with अकर्मक uses, then identify sentence type and natural word order.

## Worked example

In 'बालक ने आम खाया', आम is the object of खाया, so the verb is used transitively. In 'बालक सोया', सोया has no direct object.

## Common mistake

Do not equate every combined-looking expression with the same process. Check whether the issue is sound joining, word composition or affixation.

## Practice

What is the prefix in 'असफल'?

**Answer:** अ-, which reverses or negates the sense of सफल in this word.

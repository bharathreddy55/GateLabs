Sexual reproduction involves fusion of gametes. Asexual reproduction can occur through budding, fission or vegetative propagation depending on the organism. Seeds may disperse by wind, water, animals or mechanical release. Metamorphosis changes body form through a life cycle. During puberty, hormones coordinate patterns of physical change, and timing varies among individuals. Age-appropriate teaching supports health, dignity and respectful language.

## Worked example

A potato sprout can grow into a new plant from a tuber, demonstrating vegetative propagation. A seed developing after fertilization illustrates a different route.

## Common mistake

Growth, puberty and reproductive maturity describe overlapping but distinct processes. Avoid treating all children of one age as developing identically.

## Practice

Why is seed dispersal useful to many plants?

**Answer:** It can reduce competition near the parent and spread offspring to new suitable locations.

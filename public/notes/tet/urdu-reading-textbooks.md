The June 2026 Urdu page directs learners to Classes 3–8 textbooks. Build a table by grade and lesson with author or poet, genre, theme, purpose and relevant vocabulary. For an unfamiliar poem, identify the speaker and imagery. For unfamiliar prose, locate the sentence that supports an inference. Compare biography and literary contribution instead of treating a name as a substitute for reading the work.

## Worked example

In the sentence بچہ باغ میں گیا اور پھول دیکھے, the sequence first places the child in the garden and then describes seeing flowers. A question about location is answered from باغ میں.

## Common mistake

A lesson's theme is the larger idea developed by the text. An event is one instance of content supporting that theme.

## Practice

Why record a prose piece's literary form alongside its author?

**Answer:** Form helps predict what kinds of features to examine, such as factual sequence in biography versus invented events in a story.

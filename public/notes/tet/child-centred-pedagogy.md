Curriculum includes planned goals, content, learning experiences and assessment. Child-centred pedagogy builds on learners' prior understanding, gives them meaningful activity and checks what they learned. Cooperative learning usually assigns shared goals and individual responsibility. Reflective teachers examine evidence and revise instruction. Critical pedagogy, associated with Paulo Freire, uses dialogue and inquiry about power and experience. Gijubhai Badheka, A. S. Neill, Tomoe School and John Holt offer historical examples of alternatives to rigid instruction.

## Worked example

Before teaching measurement, ask pupils how their families estimate or measure length. Compare responses with standard units in an activity and discuss the results.

## Common mistake

An activity can be engaging without serving the intended learning goal. Check what concept or skill the pupils used.

## Practice

Why connect home experience with a concept map?

**Answer:** It makes prior ideas visible and lets the teacher connect, reorganize or address them explicitly.

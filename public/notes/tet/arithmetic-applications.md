Percentage comparisons use a stated base. Sales tax questions typically supply a rate and taxable price. Calculate a discount and tax in the order specified. Simple interest applies to original principal. Compound interest grows the accumulated balance. Direct proportion has a constant ratio, and inverse proportion a constant product.

## Worked example

A listed price of ₹500 has a 10% discount, with 5% tax on the discounted price. Discounted price is ₹450, tax ₹22.50, and final price ₹472.50.

## Common mistake

Use the taxable base provided in the problem. Do not apply a printed GST rate to a different amount by assumption.

## Practice

At simple annual interest of 5%, how much interest does ₹2,000 earn in two years?

**Answer:** ₹200, calculated as 2,000 × 0.05 × 2.

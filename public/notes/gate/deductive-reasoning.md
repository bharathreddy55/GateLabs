A deductive conclusion is valid when it must be true whenever its premises are true. Translate every premise into a direction before combining them. All A are B permits A → B. The contrapositive is not B → not A. The converse B → A needs extra information.

## Concepts in depth

A valid deduction makes the conclusion unavoidable whenever its premises hold. P → Q allows P as evidence for Q or not-Q as evidence for not-P. Q alone cannot establish P. Universal statements do not by themselves guarantee the existence of any members.

## How to apply it

From “All inspected items are tagged” and “Item X is not tagged,” X was not inspected under the rule. From “X is tagged” alone, X could have been tagged for a different reason. Use a simple counterexample to eliminate a conclusion that is only possible.

## Worked example

All clerks are employees and no employees enter the room. Therefore no clerk enters the room, because the clerk group lies inside the excluded group.

## Common mistake

Avoid affirming the consequent. If a candidate qualified, the candidate studied is a different implication from if a candidate studied, the candidate qualified.

## Practice

All roses are plants. Some plants are tall. Must some roses be tall?

**Answer:** No. The tall plants could all lie outside the set of roses.

For base a>0 and a≠1, logₐx=y exactly when aʸ=x. Product and quotient laws convert multiplication and division to addition and subtraction in the same base. A real logarithm requires a positive argument. Use exponent comparisons to estimate values between powers.

## Worked example

2⁵=32 and 2⁶=64, so log₂50 is between 5 and 6. Since 50 is above √(32×64), it is above 5.5.

## Common mistake

log(a+b) has no general decomposition as log a + log b.

## Practice

If log₂x=−3, what is x?

**Answer:** 1/8, since 2⁻³=1/8.

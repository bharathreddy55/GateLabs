For base a>0 and a≠1, logₐx=y exactly when aʸ=x. Product and quotient laws convert multiplication and division to addition and subtraction in the same base. A real logarithm requires a positive argument. Use exponent comparisons to estimate values between powers.

## Concepts in depth

For a fixed nonzero base, powers multiply by adding exponents. Logarithms require positive arguments and a positive base other than one. A log of a product splits into a sum, but a log of a sum does not split.

## How to apply it

Solving log₂(x−1)=3 gives x=9 and satisfies x>1. A base between 0 and 1 reverses inequalities because its logarithm is decreasing. Use a domain check before accepting roots from squared or rearranged equations.

## Worked example

2⁵=32 and 2⁶=64, so log₂50 is between 5 and 6. Since 50 is above √(32×64), it is above 5.5.

## Common mistake

log(a+b) has no general decomposition as log a + log b.

## Practice

If log₂x=−3, what is x?

**Answer:** 1/8, since 2⁻³=1/8.

Use the product rule for sequential choices. Ordering r distinct items from n gives nPr=n!/(n−r)!. Choosing an unordered set gives nCr=n!/(r!(n−r)!). Repeated symbols reduce the number of distinct orderings by corresponding factorials.

## Worked example

Choose a president and secretary from five people: 5×4=20. Choose a two-person committee from five: 20/2=10.

## Common mistake

Decide whether order matters before selecting a formula.

## Practice

How many distinct arrangements of LEVEL exist?

**Answer:** 30, from 5!/(2!2!) because L and E each repeat twice.

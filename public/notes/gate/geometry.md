Use angle sums and parallel-line relationships before doing arithmetic. A triangle's interior angles sum to 180°. In congruent figures corresponding lengths match, while similar triangles have proportional sides. A right triangle has a²+b²=c², with c opposite the right angle.

## Worked example

A right triangle with legs 6 and 8 has hypotenuse √(36+64)=10. A similar triangle with the shorter leg 9 has scale factor 1.5 and hypotenuse 15.

## Common mistake

An unmarked diagram may be schematic. Do not assume a side or angle is equal from appearance alone.

## Practice

A triangle has two angles 46° and 58°. Find the third.

**Answer:** 76°, since 180−46−58=76.

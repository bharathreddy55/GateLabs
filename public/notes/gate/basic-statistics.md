Mean uses all values, median is the middle of sorted values, and mode is the most frequent value. Range is maximum minus minimum. Variance averages squared deviations in the population convention, and standard deviation is its square root.

## Concepts in depth

Mean measures balance, median measures the middle ordered position, and mode is most frequent. A frequency table contributes value times frequency to total. Range depends only on extremes, while variance measures squared spread around the mean.

## How to apply it

For 2, 5, 5, 8, 20, mean=8 and median=5. Replacing 20 with 40 raises mean by 4 and leaves median unchanged. Add a constant to all observations and variance is unchanged. Multiply by c and variance scales by c².

## Worked example

For 2, 3, 3, 8, 14, the mean is 6, median and mode are 3, and range is 12. A large high value raises the mean more than the median.

## Common mistake

Sort values before finding a median. For even sample size, average the two middle values.

## Practice

Find the median of 8, 1, 3, 5.

**Answer:** 4. The two middle values after sorting are 3 and 5.

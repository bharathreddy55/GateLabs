Money serves as a medium of exchange, unit of account and store of value. Narrow and broad money use different coverage. The RBI manages monetary policy, issues currency subject to legal arrangements, acts as banker and regulates relevant institutions. The repo rate and liquidity operations influence financing conditions. Follow the latest RBI statements for rates and measures. Inflation can reflect demand, costs, supply shocks and expectations.

Fiscal policy uses spending, revenue and borrowing. Revenue deficit compares revenue expenditure with revenue receipts. Fiscal deficit is broadly total expenditure less non-debt receipts, and primary deficit deducts interest payments from fiscal deficit. Read the Union Budget and fiscal responsibility documents together. GST is a destination-based tax framework with central and state components and integrated arrangements for inter-state supplies. The balance of payments records current, capital and financial transactions with the rest of the world. Foreign direct investment is a financial-account concept associated with a lasting interest.

## Concepts in depth

Distinguish money aggregates, banking credit and the RBI policy channels affecting liquidity and rates. Demand pressures, supply shocks and expectations can drive inflation differently. Fiscal deficit includes the borrowing requirement under the standard budget definition. Primary deficit removes interest payments. External accounts separate current flows from financial investments.

## How to apply it

With expenditure 8,200, revenue receipts 6,500, non-debt capital receipts 450 and interest 620, fiscal deficit is 1,250 and primary deficit 630. In external accounts do not put FDI into the current account. For GST and bank policy questions use definitions in the relevant official publication and date changes.

## Worked example

Expenditure of 1,000 and non-debt receipts of 850 give a fiscal deficit of 150. If interest payments are 40, primary deficit is 110.

## Common mistake

A fiscal deficit is a flow for a period. Public debt is a stock measured at a date.

## Practice

Where do foreign direct investment flows ordinarily appear in balance of payments accounting?

**Answer:** FDI is recorded under direct investment in the financial account.

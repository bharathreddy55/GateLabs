Study technology through the institution, mission, application and public policy. ISRO's launch and spacecraft programmes serve communications, navigation, observation and exploration. Chandrayaan-3 demonstrated lunar landing and roving. Aditya-L1 is a solar-observation mission. Distinguish a launch vehicle from its payload and the mission from its individual instruments. DRDO is a government defence research organisation. The IGMDP is a historical programme to place in its institutional and policy context.

Digital India and e-governance involve infrastructure, digital identity, public services, interoperability, access and privacy. Cybersecurity study at this level centres on governance, reporting, authentication, resilience and incident response. Civil nuclear topics include reactor types, generation, safety institutions and non-power applications such as diagnostic and therapeutic radioisotopes. For innovation policies, emerging technologies and recent missions, use dated publications from the issuing organisations.

## Concepts in depth

Group topics by public mission rather than acronym alone. ISRO space work includes launch services, remote sensing and scientific missions. Docking and electrical transfer, for example, demonstrate different capabilities. Public digital platforms, governance and data protection have separate roles. Nuclear energy and radioisotopes use different applications of radiation.

## How to apply it

Match department, mission objective and verified milestone. For remote sensing, distinguish the sensor, orbit and end use. For a public service platform, separate identity, payment, information and grievance mechanisms. Track a mission's announcement, trial, operational milestone and evaluation as different stages.

## Worked example

Match Chandrayaan-3 to lunar surface operations and Aditya-L1 to solar observations. The mission objective is the fastest way to rule out a plausible-looking distractor.

## Common mistake

A rocket and the satellite or probe it carries are different parts of a launch.

## Practice

What is Aditya-L1 primarily designed to study?

**Answer:** Aditya-L1 is India's observatory mission for studying the Sun.

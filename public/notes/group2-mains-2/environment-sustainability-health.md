Greenhouse gas accumulation warms the climate. Ocean uptake of carbon dioxide lowers seawater pH. Ozone layer depletion concerns stratospheric chemistry, and acid deposition is linked primarily to pollutants such as sulphur dioxide and nitrogen oxides. Match each problem to mechanism and policy. The Montreal Protocol addresses ozone-depleting substances. The Paris Agreement addresses climate action through nationally determined contributions. Adaptation and mitigation tackle different dimensions of risk.

Sustainable development considers social, economic and environmental outcomes. The UN Sustainable Development Goals frame targets and monitoring. Public health compares incidence, prevalence, deaths and disability, accounting for data and access. Outbreak preparedness includes surveillance, communication, laboratory and healthcare capacity, coordination and equitable access. ## Worked example

Protecting a flood-prone clinic with reliable backup services reduces climate vulnerability. Cutting emissions from energy production is mitigation.

Agricultural production equals area multiplied by yield. Crop choice depends on water, season, prices and local suitability. Farm credit and marketing affect access to inputs, storage and buyers. MSP is a support-price announcement, procurement is actual government purchase under applicable arrangements, and issue prices relate to distribution. Read food stocks and distribution as separate stages from farm production.

Industry contributes to investment, jobs, productivity and regional development. The 1991 industrial policy reduced licensing across many activities. Disinvestment concerns sale of government stakes. Industrial sickness can result from financial strain, outdated technology, weak demand or management problems. Business entry, compliance, infrastructure and finance affect firms. Services include trade, transport, finance and IT/ITES. Compare employment, productivity, output and export contributions rather than relying on a single sector share.

## Worked example

If cultivated area stays at 100 hectares and yield rises from 2 to 3 tonnes per hectare, production rises from 200 to 300 tonnes because productivity increased.

## Common mistake

An announced MSP and realised farmer prices are different measures. Procurement coverage varies.

## Practice

Output rises with area unchanged. Which measure must have risen?

**Answer:** With a fixed area the change in output comes from higher output per unit of area.

## Concepts in depth

Distinguish source reduction, segregation, recycling, treatment and disposal. Wastewater indicators such as BOD describe specific parameters. Successive removal efficiencies multiply their remaining fractions. Pollution exposure depends on pathway, concentration and time. Bioremediation uses biological processes to address some contaminants under suitable conditions.

## How to apply it

If incoming BOD is 240 mg/L and two stages remove 30% and then 85% of their own inlets, the final concentration is 240×0.70×0.15=25.2 mg/L at constant flow. Avoid adding the two efficiencies. Match the process to the pollutant and check residuals.

## Worked example

Separate wet organics at collection and send them to appropriate biological treatment. Mixing them into dry recyclables contaminates both streams.

## Common mistake

A remediation method that works for one contaminant and site does not automatically work elsewhere.

## Practice

Which method specifically uses biological activity to manage contaminants?

**Answer:** Bioremediation relies on suitable organisms or biological processes to transform, remove or immobilise pollutants.

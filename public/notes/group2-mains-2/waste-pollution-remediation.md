The waste hierarchy prioritises prevention, reuse and recycling before final disposal. Segregation separates biodegradable materials, recyclables, hazardous household materials and other streams for suitable treatment. Compare composting and anaerobic digestion for organic matter, recycling for suitable materials, and engineered disposal for residuals. Waste methods entail location, operating and emission considerations.

Air, water, soil and noise pollutants have different sources, pathways and impacts. Pollution control begins with source reduction and then uses treatment and monitoring. Study the roles of pollution control boards and the relevant Indian rules. Environmental assessment of transgenic crops considers trait, crop, exposure, gene flow and receiving environment within the regulatory framework. Eco-friendly agriculture includes soil cover, integrated management and resource efficiency. Bioremediation uses biological processes to manage contaminants under suitable site conditions.

## Worked example

Separate wet organics at collection and send them to appropriate biological treatment. Mixing them into dry recyclables contaminates both streams.

## Common mistake

A remediation method that works for one contaminant and site does not automatically work elsewhere.

## Practice

Which method specifically uses biological activity to manage contaminants?

**Answer:** Bioremediation relies on suitable organisms or biological processes to transform, remove or immobilise pollutants.

## What it means

A measurement records a numerical value and a unit. A physical quantity keeps the same value when expressed in a different unit, even though the number changes. SI units give a common reference, including metre for length, kilogram for mass, second for time and kelvin for thermodynamic temperature.

## Formulas and rules

- **Metric prefixes:** kilo=10³, centi=10⁻², milli=10⁻³, micro=10⁻⁶. Thus 1 m=100 cm and 1 cm=10 mm.
- **Derived units:** Multiply or divide base units just as you do the quantities. The newton is kg·m/s² and the joule is N·m.
- **Area and volume:** Square or cube a length conversion. 1 cm²=10⁻⁴ m² and 1 cm³=10⁻⁶ m³. 1 L=10⁻³ m³.
- **Precision:** Read the least scale division and account for any zero offset. Correct reading=indicated reading−zero reading for an additive zero error.

## How to study it for Station Master

- Write conversion factors as fractions that cancel the original unit, especially with km/h and density. Estimate whether the new numeric value should increase or decrease.
- Review base and derived units together. Practise one conversion with powers, one with a compound unit, and one reading with a zero error.

## Question models and worked examples

### Convert volume units

**Question.** Convert 0.036 m³ to litres and cm³.

1. Multiply cubic metres by 1,000 for litres.
2. Multiply litres by 1,000 for cubic centimetres.

**Answer:** 36 L or 36,000 cm³.

### Convert a compound unit

**Question.** Convert 2.7 g/cm³ to kg/m³.

1. 2.7 g=0.0027 kg and 1 cm³=0.000001 m³.
2. Divide 0.0027 by 0.000001.

**Answer:** 2700 kg/m³.

### Identify derived dimensions

**Question.** Express one joule in SI base units.

1. A joule equals a newton-metre.
2. Replace N with kg·m/s² and multiply by one more metre.

**Answer:** kg·m²/s².

### Correct zero error

**Question.** A caliper reads +0.03 cm when closed and 1.82 cm on a rod. What is the corrected diameter?

1. The positive zero reading means every indicated reading is 0.03 cm too high in this model.
2. Subtract 0.03 cm from 1.82 cm.

**Answer:** 1.79 cm.

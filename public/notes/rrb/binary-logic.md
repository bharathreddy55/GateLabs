A proposition has a truth value. NOT reverses it, AND needs both inputs true, and inclusive OR needs at least one true. A truth table tests every input combination. For truth-teller puzzles, assign each claim true or false and reject inconsistent cases.

## Worked example

P is true and Q false. P AND Q is false, P OR Q is true, and NOT Q is true.

## Common mistake

Ordinary OR in formal logic is inclusive unless the problem explicitly says exactly one.

## Practice

When is P XOR Q true?

**Answer:** Exactly when one of P and Q is true.

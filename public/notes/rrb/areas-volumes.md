## What it means

Area measures flat coverage in square units. Surface area measures the exposed faces of a solid. Volume measures enclosed space in cubic units. For paint or wrapping count exposed surfaces, for storage or water use interior volume, and for a path use the outer region minus its cutout.

## Formulas and rules

- **Flat figures:** Rectangle A=lw, triangle A=bh/2 for perpendicular height, trapezium A=(a+b)h/2 for parallel bases a,b, circle A=πr². Sector area is θ/360° of a circle.
- **Prisms and cylinders:** V=base area×height. Cylinder curved area=2πrh, closed total area=2πr(h+r). Cuboid exposed area uses two faces of each product lw,lh,wh unless a face is open or covered.
- **Cones and spheres:** Cone V=πr²h/3 and curved area=πrℓ for slant height ℓ. Sphere V=4πr³/3 and area=4πr².
- **Units:** 1 m²=10,000 cm², 1 m³=1,000,000 cm³, 1 L=1,000 cm³. Square or cube the conversion factor.

## How to study it for Station Master

- Underline the object and quantity: area, perimeter, surface or capacity. Draw the opening, the inner cutout and any face shared by solids.
- Practise a composite area, a missing-face surface, a unit conversion and water transfer. For a solid scaled by k, check the answer scales by k² or k³ as appropriate.

## Question models and worked examples

### Path around a rectangle

**Question.** A 20 m by 14 m garden has a 1 m wide path outside all four edges. Find path area.

1. Outer dimensions are 22 by 16 because the path adds 1 on both sides of each dimension.
2. Subtract the garden: 22×16−20×14=352−280.

**Answer:** 72 m².

### Annular sector

**Question.** A 90° sector has outer radius 10 cm and inner radius 6 cm removed. Find remaining area.

1. Full annulus area is π(100−36)=64π cm².
2. A right angle is one-quarter of a full turn, so take a quarter of the ring.

**Answer:** 16π cm².

### Open cylindrical container

**Question.** An open-top cylinder has radius 7 cm and height 10 cm. How much sheet area is needed, ignoring overlaps?

1. Curved area is 2π×7×10=140π cm².
2. Add one base of area 49π, leaving the top open.

**Answer:** 189π cm².

### Transfer and capacity

**Question.** A tank base is 80 by 50 cm. Pour in 24 L. How far does the water level rise?

1. 24 L=24,000 cm³. Base area=4,000 cm².
2. Divide added volume by base area.

**Answer:** 6 cm.

### Comparing solids in the same space

**Question.** A cone and a cylinder have radius 3 cm and height 8 cm. What volume is unfilled if the cone’s contents are poured into the cylinder?

1. Cylinder capacity=π×9×8=72π cm³.
2. Equal-base, equal-height cone contains one-third, 24π cm³. Subtract from 72π.

**Answer:** 48π cm³.

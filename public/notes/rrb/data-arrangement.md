Turn conditions into a grid or ordering and record fixed placements first. Track each condition with a separate mark. Branch only when a choice has multiple feasible cases. Check every remaining case against all conditions before choosing a conclusion.

## Worked example

Three trains A, B and C depart in distinct slots. A before B and C before A force C–A–B by transitivity.

## Common mistake

Adjacent means consecutive positions. Before alone allows intervening entries.

## Practice

If A precedes B and B precedes C, must A be immediately before B?

**Answer:** No. An extra event may occur between A and B unless the question requires adjacency.

Identify the problem, central claim and how each paragraph contributes. A main idea should cover the passage's central scope. A supporting idea can give evidence, a mechanism, a comparison or an example. These tasks apply across history, society, literature, science, environment, abstract subjects, mythology, technology and culture.

## Worked example

Passage: A city's rain gardens store stormwater and reduce pressure on drainage. A pilot garden on one street cut runoff during several storms. The first sentence states the main idea, and the pilot is supporting evidence.

## Common mistake

A striking example may be too narrow for a main-idea answer.

## Practice

If a passage argues that oral traditions preserve local environmental knowledge and illustrates this with a myth about seasonal floods, which is the main idea?

**Answer:** Oral traditions can preserve environmental knowledge. The myth is an example.

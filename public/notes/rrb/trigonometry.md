For an acute angle in a right triangle, sin = opposite/hypotenuse, cos = adjacent/hypotenuse and tan = opposite/adjacent. The labels opposite and adjacent depend on the selected angle. For height questions, include the observer's height if the figure requires it.

## Concepts in depth

For acute angle θ in a right triangle, sin is opposite/hypotenuse, cos is adjacent/hypotenuse and tan is opposite/adjacent. The side labels change when the reference angle changes. sin²θ+cos²θ=1. Use a right triangle before applying the ratios.

## How to apply it

If a mast's height is 12 m and the horizontal distance is 16 m, tan θ=3/4 and cos θ=4/5 because the hypotenuse is 20 m. In an elevation problem, align the base with the observer's horizontal sightline.

## Worked example

At level ground 20 m from a tower, the angle of elevation to its top from the ground is 45°. Height = 20 tan 45° = 20 m.

## Common mistake

Do not use the line of sight as the adjacent side. It is the hypotenuse.

## Practice

A right triangle has opposite side 3 and hypotenuse 5. Find cos θ.

**Answer:** 4/5. Pythagoras gives adjacent side 4.

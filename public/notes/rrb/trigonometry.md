## What it means

Trigonometry connects angles and side lengths. In a right triangle, the hypotenuse is opposite the right angle. Relative to a chosen acute angle, the opposite leg faces it and the adjacent leg touches it. Choosing the other acute angle swaps those two legs.

## Formulas and rules

- **Ratios:** sin θ=opposite/hypotenuse, cos θ=adjacent/hypotenuse, tan θ=opposite/adjacent. Also tan θ=sin θ/cos θ for nonzero cosine.
- **Exact values:** For 30°,45°,60° the sines are 1/2, √2/2, √3/2, cosines in reverse order, and tangents 1/√3,1,√3.
- **Pythagorean identity:** sin²θ+cos²θ=1. Both ratios are positive for acute θ.
- **Elevation and depression:** Both are measured from a horizontal reference. Parallel horizontals give corresponding elevation and depression angles for the same line of sight.

## How to study it for Station Master

- Draw the right angle and label the reference angle before choosing a ratio. Use tan when only vertical and horizontal distances are involved.
- Mix exact values, missing ratios and observer-height scenarios. Keep radicals exact until the problem asks for rounding.

## Question models and worked examples

### Find remaining ratios

**Question.** Given sin θ=5/13 for acute θ, find cos θ and tan θ.

1. Take opposite=5, hypotenuse=13 and adjacent=√(169−25)=12.
2. Use adjacent/hypotenuse and opposite/adjacent for the same angle.

**Answer:** 12/13 and 5/12.

### Observer height

**Question.** A person’s eye level is 1.6 m above the ground. From 18 m away the pole top has elevation 45°. Find the pole height.

1. Opposite rise above eye level is 18×tan 45°=18 m.
2. Add the 1.6 m between the eye line and ground.

**Answer:** 19.6 m.

### Two stations of observation

**Question.** From ground level on the same side, angles of elevation to a tower top are 60° and 30°. Observation points are 20 m apart. Find the height.

1. For nearer distance x, height=x√3. At the farther point it is (x+20)/√3.
2. Equality gives 3x=x+20, so x=10 m.

**Answer:** 10√3 m.

### Exact value and identity

**Question.** Evaluate 1−sin²30°+tan²45°.

1. Use sin 30°=1/2 and tan 45°=1.
2. Square before subtracting: 1−1/4+1.

**Answer:** 7/4.

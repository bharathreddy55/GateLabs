The highest common factor divides every input. The least common multiple is divisible by every input. Prime factorization uses minimum exponents for HCF and maximum exponents for LCM. For positive a and b, HCF × LCM = a × b.

## Concepts in depth

HCF is the largest shared divisor and LCM the smallest positive shared multiple. The Euclidean algorithm preserves gcd when a pair is replaced by the smaller number and the remainder. For two positive integers, product equals HCF×LCM.

## How to apply it

Recurring events at 12- and 18-minute intervals align after LCM=36 minutes if both start together. For largest equal pack size from both stock counts use HCF=6. If events have different start offsets, the LCM gives repetition after any alignment, not necessarily the first alignment.

## Worked example

Two signals repeat every 12 and 18 seconds and align now. The LCM is 36, so their next alignment happens 36 seconds later.

## Common mistake

Use the HCF for the biggest equal cutting length and the LCM for repeating events meeting again.

## Practice

What is the largest equal length that exactly divides rods of 48 cm and 60 cm?

**Answer:** 12 cm, the HCF of 48 and 60.

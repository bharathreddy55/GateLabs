## What it means

A simple machine changes the size or direction of an effort force. It trades force against distance and cannot deliver more energy than it receives. A lever turns about a fulcrum. Compare moments about that fulcrum using perpendicular lever arms. Pulleys and slopes can similarly reduce the required effort by increasing the distance it acts over.

## Formulas and rules

- **Lever equilibrium:** Effort×perpendicular effort arm = load×perpendicular load arm for a balanced ideal lever with neglected lever weight.
- **Lever classes:** Class I has fulcrum in the middle, class II has load in the middle, class III has effort in the middle.
- **Machine measures:** MA=load/effort, VR=effort distance/load distance, efficiency=MA/VR as a fraction.
- **Ideal examples:** A fixed pulley mainly changes direction and gives MA=1. For a simple ideal block and tackle count supporting rope segments on the moving load. An ideal ramp has VR=length/vertical rise.

## How to study it for Station Master

- Identify fulcrum, load and effort before computing or assigning class. For pulleys count rope segments actually supporting the moving assembly.
- Do both force and distance checks. If a result gives MA greater than VR for an ordinary passive machine, revisit input, output and friction assumptions.

## Question models and worked examples

### Lever moments

**Question.** A 300 N load acts 0.2 m from the pivot. How much perpendicular effort at 0.75 m on the opposite side balances it?

1. Load moment=300×0.2=60 N·m.
2. Balance moments: effort=60/0.75.

**Answer:** 80 N.

### Lever class from layout

**Question.** In a wheelbarrow the wheel axle is the fulcrum, load sits between axle and hands, and hands lift the handles. Classify it.

1. Read the order along the bar: fulcrum → load → effort.
2. The position of the load in the middle defines the class.

**Answer:** Class II.

### Inclined plane efficiency

**Question.** A 240 N load rises 1 m along a 5 m ramp using 60 N along the ramp. Find MA and efficiency.

1. MA=240/60=4. VR=5/1=5.
2. Efficiency=4/5, which also equals 240 J useful output ÷ 300 J input.

**Answer:** MA 4, efficiency 80%.

### Pulley with loss

**Question.** A moving block is supported by four rope segments. The 320 N load needs 100 N effort and rises 2 m. Find rope pulled and efficiency.

1. VR=4, so pull distance is 4×2=8 m.
2. Output work is 640 J and input work is 800 J. Divide.

**Answer:** 8 m pulled, efficiency 80%.

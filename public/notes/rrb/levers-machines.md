A lever rotates about a fulcrum. Torque magnitude is force times perpendicular distance from the axis. For equilibrium, clockwise and anticlockwise torques balance. Simple machines, including levers, pulleys and inclined planes, trade effort force against distance in the ideal case. Real machines lose energy to friction and deformation.

## Worked example

A load of 60 N acts 0.2 m from a pivot. At a perpendicular effort arm of 0.6 m, balance requires 60×0.2/0.6=20 N.

## Common mistake

Use the perpendicular moment arm, not simply any distance between the point of application and the pivot.

## Practice

Why does an ideal longer ramp require less force to raise the same mass?

**Answer:** The work to gain height is spread over a longer travel distance.

## What it means

Descriptive statistics summarise measurements. Mean uses every numeric value, median tracks the middle rank after sorting, mode tracks frequency, and range measures the gap between smallest and largest. A single unusual value can move the mean and range substantially while the median remains stable.

## Formulas and rules

- **Frequency mean:** x̄=Σ(fx)/Σf. Frequency f counts how many copies of a value x occur.
- **Median:** For odd n take sorted position (n+1)/2. For even n average positions n/2 and n/2+1.
- **Mode and range:** Mode is any value of highest frequency, with ties possible. Range=max−min.
- **Rescaling:** Adding c to all observations moves mean and median by c and leaves range unchanged. Multiplying by positive k scales mean, median and range by k.

## How to study it for Station Master

- Read the requested statistic before doing arithmetic. For a frequency table build cumulative frequencies to locate the median rather than writing every copy.
- Practise comparing centres after adding an outlier. Keep an eye on total frequency and the two middle positions for an even number of readings.

## Question models and worked examples

### Mean from frequencies

**Question.** Values 2, 5 and 8 occur 3, 4 and 1 times. Find the mean.

1. Weighted total=2×3+5×4+8×1=34.
2. Total frequency is 8. Divide 34 by 8.

**Answer:** 4.25.

### Median and mode from a table

**Question.** Values 4, 7, 9 and 12 occur 3, 2, 3 and 2 times. Find median and modes.

1. The ten sorted entries have middle positions 5 and 6. Cumulative frequencies 3,5,8,10 place them at 7 and 9.
2. Average the middle positions. The largest frequency is 3, tied by 4 and 9.

**Answer:** Median 8, modes 4 and 9.

### Sensitivity to an outlier

**Question.** For 2,3,4,5,6 replace 6 by 36. Compare mean and median.

1. The old total is 20. Replacing 6 adds 30, so the new mean is 50/5=10 instead of 4.
2. The middle position is still 4. Only the largest value changed.

**Answer:** Mean rises by 6, median stays 4.

### Translate readings

**Question.** Readings have mean 18 and range 7. Add 12 to every reading. Find mean and range.

1. The total rises by 12 times the count, so division by count raises the mean by 12.
2. The minimum and maximum both rise by 12, cancelling in their difference.

**Answer:** Mean 30, range 7.

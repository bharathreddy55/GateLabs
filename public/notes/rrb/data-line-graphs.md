## What it means

A line graph shows how a quantity varies along a second variable, often time. Level describes the value at a point, while slope measures change per unit of the horizontal variable. A flat segment means the plotted value stayed constant across that segment. A line connecting observations does not always indicate what happened between them.

## Formulas and rules

- **Change:** y₂−y₁ is absolute change; (y₂−y₁)/y₁ gives relative change for nonzero y₁.
- **Slope:** (y₂−y₁)/(x₂−x₁), with units y per x. Compare rates after allowing for different time gaps.
- **Cumulative line:** Increments are endpoint differences. A plateau means zero increase in the cumulative measure over that period.
- **Interpolation:** A straight-line estimate between observations assumes change at a constant rate within that segment.

## How to study it for Station Master

- Ask whether the question wants the highest value, largest rise or fastest rise. Extract pairs of coordinates with the right series and units.
- Train with uneven time gaps and cumulative values. For crossing or midpoint questions state the assumed linear segment before interpolating.

## Question models and worked examples

### Steepest rise with uneven intervals

**Question.** Figures are 90 on day 0, 110 on day 2 and 134 on day 6. Which interval has faster daily growth?

1. Days 0–2: change 20 over two days, or 10/day.
2. Days 2–6: change 24 over four days, or 6/day.

**Answer:** Days 0–2.

### Net change versus interval change

**Question.** A series reads 80 in April, 100 in May and 90 in June. Find net percent change April to June.

1. Use April 80 and June 90. The intermediate peak does not affect the net calculation.
2. Change is 10/80×100%.

**Answer:** 12.5% increase.

### Rate from a cumulative graph

**Question.** Cumulative calls rise from 50 at 9:00 to 170 at 11:00 and 245 at 12:00. Which interval had more calls per hour?

1. 9–11 added 120 calls over two hours, or 60 per hour.
2. 11–12 added 75 in one hour.

**Answer:** 11–12.

### Interpolation

**Question.** A straight segment connects (10 min, 40 L) and (18 min, 72 L). Estimate volume at 15 min.

1. Slope=(72−40)/(18−10)=4 L/min.
2. 15 is five minutes after the first point, so add 5×4 to 40.

**Answer:** 60 L.

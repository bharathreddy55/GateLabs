## What it means

Plane geometry studies flat figures. Diagrams help track incidences, but equal-looking sides and angles only count when given or proved. Similar figures have matching angles and proportional corresponding lengths. Congruent figures also have the same size.

## Formulas and rules

- **Angles:** A straight angle is 180°, a full turn 360°, and vertical opposite angles are equal. Parallel lines give equal corresponding or alternate angles with a transversal.
- **Triangles:** Interior angles sum to 180°. An exterior angle equals the sum of the two remote interior angles. Use c²=a²+b² only for a right triangle and hypotenuse c.
- **Similarity:** AA gives triangle similarity; length scale k gives area scale k². SSS, SAS with the included angle, and ASA establish congruence.
- **Polygons and tangents:** Interior sum=(n−2)×180°. The exterior turn of a regular n-gon is 360°/n. A circle’s radius meets its tangent at 90°.

## How to study it for Station Master

- Mark parallel lines, right angles and vertex order before choosing a theorem. Match like sides as a separate step in a similar-triangle example.
- Practise a parallel-line triangle, a polygon-angle reversal and a tangent right triangle. Make a fresh sketch whose proportions do not suggest the answer.

## Question models and worked examples

### Exterior angle with a ratio

**Question.** A triangle’s exterior angle is 132°. Its remote interior angles are in ratio 5:6. Find the three interior angles.

1. The two remote angles total 132°, so each ratio part is 12°. The two are 60° and 72°.
2. The remaining interior angle is supplementary to 132°, so it is 48°.

**Answer:** 60°, 72°, 48°.

### Parallel line and area

**Question.** In ABC, D is on AB and E on AC with DE parallel to BC. AD:DB=2:3. Area of ABC is 125 cm². Find area of ADE.

1. Parallelism gives two matching angles, hence similarity.
2. Length scale is AD/AB=2/5. Multiply 125 by (2/5)².

**Answer:** 20 cm².

### Regular polygon

**Question.** A regular polygon has an interior angle of 156°. Find its sides.

1. Exterior turn=180°−156°=24°.
2. A full circuit turns 360°, so count=360/24.

**Answer:** 15.

### Tangent length

**Question.** Distance from external point P to circle centre O is 13 cm, with radius 5 cm. Find the length to a touchpoint T.

1. OT meets PT at 90°. OP is the hypotenuse.
2. PT²=13²−5²=144. Take the positive root.

**Answer:** 12 cm.

Speed measures distance travelled per unit time and has no direction. Average velocity is displacement divided by time, with direction. Relative velocity of A with respect to B is vA−vB. Assign a sign convention before subtracting along a line.

## Worked example

A train moves east at 18 m/s and another east at 11 m/s. The first has velocity +7 m/s relative to the second. If the second goes west at 11 m/s, the relative value is +29 m/s.

## Common mistake

An out-and-back trip can have zero average velocity and positive average speed.

## Practice

On a straight line, A moves east at 5 m/s and B west at 3 m/s. Find A relative to B.

**Answer:** 8 m/s east, using +5−(−3).

## What it means

Distance counts path length, while displacement measures the change in position with direction. Speed is a scalar and velocity has direction. Average velocity depends only on displacement over the interval. Relative velocity measures how the position of one object changes as seen from another moving frame.

## Formulas and rules

- **Average quantities:** Speed=total distance/elapsed time, velocity=displacement/elapsed time. An out-and-back trip returns to zero displacement.
- **Relative velocity:** v(A relative to B)=vA−vB with signed directions or vectors. Closing speed is the magnitude of change of separation while approaching.
- **Uniform acceleration:** a=(v−u)/t, v=u+at, s=ut+at²/2. These forms assume constant acceleration and signed variables in the same axis.
- **Velocity-time graph:** Its slope gives acceleration and signed area gives displacement. Count magnitudes of areas below zero for distance.

## How to study it for Station Master

- Choose and mark a positive direction before setting numbers. Practise turning a negative number into a physical direction in the answer.
- Work a return trip, same- and opposite-direction comparisons, and a staged velocity graph. Separate acceleration from velocity and compare slopes before doing arithmetic.

## Question models and worked examples

### Average speed and velocity

**Question.** Run 80 m east then 50 m west in 26 s. Find the average speed and velocity.

1. Total distance is 130 m, so average speed=130/26.
2. Displacement is 30 m east, so average velocity=30/26 east.

**Answer:** 5 m/s speed and 15/13 m/s east velocity.

### Signed relative velocity

**Question.** A travels east at 18 m/s and B west at 12 m/s. What is B’s velocity relative to A?

1. With east positive, vA=+18 and vB=−12.
2. vB relative to A=−12−18=−30 m/s.

**Answer:** 30 m/s west.

### Constant braking

**Question.** A vehicle slows uniformly from 20 m/s to rest in 5 s. Find acceleration and distance.

1. Choose the motion direction as positive. a=(0−20)/5=−4 m/s².
2. Mean velocity during uniform braking is 10 m/s. Multiply by 5 s.

**Answer:** −4 m/s² and 50 m.

### Signed area of a velocity graph

**Question.** An object moves at +4 m/s for 3 s and then −2 m/s for 5 s. Find displacement and distance.

1. The signed rectangular areas are +12 m and −10 m. Sum them for displacement.
2. Sum the magnitudes for path length.

**Answer:** +2 m displacement, 22 m distance.

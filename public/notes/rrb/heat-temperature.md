Temperature indicates thermal state, while heat is energy transferred because of a temperature difference. For a temperature change within one phase, Q=mcΔT. During a phase change at fixed pressure, Q=mL gives the energy for the transformation. Conduction, convection and radiation transfer thermal energy through different mechanisms.

## Concepts in depth

Temperature describes thermal state and heat is energy transferred due to temperature difference. For heating without a phase change, Q=mcΔT. For mixing in an insulated system, heat lost equals heat gained after including any stated container heat capacity.

## How to apply it

Mix 200 g water at 70°C and 300 g at 20°C in an insulated negligible-capacity vessel. Equal specific heat gives (200×70+300×20)/500=40°C. Adding temperatures without mass weights gives an incorrect equilibrium.

## Worked example

Heating 0.5 kg water by 10 K takes about 0.5×4,200×10=21,000 J, neglecting losses.

## Common mistake

A Celsius temperature and a kelvin temperature have different zero points, while a change of 1 °C has the same size as a change of 1 K.

## Practice

Why can ice absorb heat while staying at its melting temperature?

**Answer:** Energy goes into changing the phase rather than increasing the temperature of the ice-water mixture.

An arithmetic progression has a constant difference d. Its nth term is a+(n−1)d and the sum of its first n terms is n(a+last)/2. The difference may be negative or zero.

## Worked example

For 5, 8, 11, …, term 12 is 5+11×3=38. The sum of the first 12 terms is 12(5+38)/2=258.

## Common mistake

There are n−1 intervals between the first and nth terms.

## Practice

Find the 8th term of 32, 29, 26, ….

**Answer:** 11, using 32+7×(−3).

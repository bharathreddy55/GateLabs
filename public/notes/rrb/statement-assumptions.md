An unstated assumption bridges a claim and the proposed result. Negate a candidate assumption and check whether the reasoning still works. The bridge often concerns feasibility, causation or audience response.

## Worked example

The school will add a late bus so more students can attend clubs. If transport after clubs is no barrier for any student, the proposed reason for improved participation weakens.

## Common mistake

A generally true statement qualifies only if the argument actually relies on it.

## Practice

A library extends evening hours to increase access for workers. What assumption links the decision to the goal?

**Answer:** Some workers who cannot visit during old hours can use the extension.

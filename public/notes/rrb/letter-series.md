Write letters as positions A = 1 through Z = 26. Compare differences or multiple tracks within each term. Use wrap-around only if the pattern indicates it.

## Concepts in depth

Convert letters to positions. For multi-letter terms, compare each column and look for opposite directions, fixed gaps, increasing steps or interleaving. Treat wraparound as arithmetic modulo 26 while keeping the position convention consistent.

## How to apply it

AZ, CX, FU, JQ increases the first letter by 2,3,4 and decreases the second letter by the same amounts. Next steps +5 and −5 yield OL. Similar-looking letters need not be adjacent in alphabetic order.

## Worked example

A, C, F, J, O has steps +2, +3, +4 and +5. Add 6 to position 15 to reach 21, or U.

## Common mistake

A group such as AZ, BY, CX can contain an increasing and a decreasing sequence. Track each column.

## Practice

Complete B, E, H, K, __.

**Answer:** N, by a constant step of three positions.

Classify integers, rational and irrational numbers, and use place value, factorization and remainders. Divisibility by 3 or 9 depends on digit sum. For LCM take the maximum exponent of each prime across the numbers. For HCF take the minimum, counting a missing exponent as zero.

## Worked example

18 = 2 × 3² and 24 = 2³ × 3. The HCF is 2 × 3 = 6. The LCM is 2³ × 3² = 72.

## Common mistake

Zero is an integer, and division by zero is undefined. A decimal that terminates or repeats is rational.

## Practice

What remainder does 428 leave on division by 9?

**Answer:** 5. Its digit sum is 14, which is congruent to 5 modulo 9.

Draw the seating positions and choose an orientation. Mark immediate neighbours and opposite positions before assigning flexible clues. In a circle, fixing one participant's location removes rotational duplicates when absolute seats do not matter.

## Worked example

Four people sit around a circular table, with A opposite B and C immediately clockwise from A. Fix A first, place B two seats away, put C in the next clockwise slot and use the final slot for D.

## Common mistake

When people face inward, their personal left or right depends on where they sit. A clockwise direction belongs to the whole diagram.

## Practice

If A and B are at opposite positions among six equally spaced seats, how many seats are between them on either side?

**Answer:** Two seats on each side, since opposite positions are three steps apart.

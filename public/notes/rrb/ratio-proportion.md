## What it means

A ratio compares amounts measured in compatible units. A:B=a:b means they can be expressed as ak and bk. A proportion says two ratios are equal. A direct relationship keeps the ratio of two variables fixed, and an inverse relationship keeps their product fixed.

## Formulas and rules

- **Part of a total:** If A:B=a:b, A/(A+B)=a/(a+b). A/B=a/b compares two parts.
- **Cross-multiplication:** a/b=c/d implies ad=bc for nonzero b and d.
- **Direct and inverse variation:** y=kx and xy=k respectively. Crew size and time are inverse for a fixed workload and equal output per worker.
- **Linked ratios:** Match the number of units of the shared quantity before combining.

## How to study it for Station Master

- Write each ratio in words, including which moment it describes. Mark the common entry in a linked ratio.
- For transfers write initial amounts as multiples, then apply both changes. For mixed variation keep a concrete output unit, such as metres per worker-day.

## Question models and worked examples

### Linked ratios

**Question.** A:B=3:4 and B:C=6:5. Split ₹1,860 between them.

1. **Match B in both ratios.** B represents 4 parts in A:B and 6 in B:C. Their least common multiple is 12. Multiply the first ratio by 3 and the second by 2 to get A:B = 9:12 and B:C = 12:10.
2. **Find the value of a part.** The combined ratio is 9:12:10, so the full amount is 31 parts. Divide ₹1,860 by 31 to get ₹60 per part.
3. **Allocate the amount.** Multiply ₹60 by 9, 12 and 10. The resulting shares add back to ₹1,860.

**Answer:** ₹540, ₹720, ₹600.

### Ratio after a transfer

**Question.** Stocks are in ratio 7:5. Transfer 24 units from the first to the second and they become equal. Find the originals.

1. **Track the original stocks.** Write the stocks as 7k and 5k. A transfer of 24 changes the first to 7k − 24 and the second to 5k + 24.
2. **Equate the new amounts.** Solve 7k − 24 = 5k + 24. This gives 2k = 48 and k = 24.
3. **Recover and verify the originals.** Calculate 7 × 24 = 168 and 5 × 24 = 120. After transferring 24, both become 144.

**Answer:** 168 and 120.

### Inverse and direct variation

**Question.** Eight workers mark 120 m in six days. How many days for 12 workers to mark 210 m at the same output per worker?

1. **Find the daily rate per worker.** Eight workers over six days supply 48 worker-days. At the same output per person, one worker-day produces 120 ÷ 48 = 2.5 m.
2. **Convert the new job to worker-days.** At 2.5 m per worker-day, marking 210 m takes 210 ÷ 2.5 = 84 worker-days.
3. **Share the work across 12 workers.** The 12 workers supply 12 worker-days each calendar day, so the job takes 84 ÷ 12 = 7 days.

**Answer:** 7 days.

### Weighted mixture

**Question.** Mix 30% and 50% solutions in a 3:2 mass ratio. Find the resulting concentration.

1. **Choose a convenient total mass.** Take 3 kg of the 30% mixture and 2 kg of the 50% mixture. The ratio is by mass, so the combined mass is 5 kg.
2. **Add the solute from each portion.** The first contributes 3 × 0.30 = 0.9 kg and the second contributes 2 × 0.50 = 1 kg, giving 1.9 kg of solute.
3. **Divide by the whole mixture.** Concentration by mass is solute mass ÷ total mass × 100%. Here 1.9 ÷ 5 × 100% = 38%.

**Answer:** 38%.

## What it means

A ratio compares amounts measured in compatible units. A:B=a:b means they can be expressed as ak and bk. A proportion says two ratios are equal. A direct relationship keeps the ratio of two variables fixed, and an inverse relationship keeps their product fixed.

## Formulas and rules

- **Part of a total:** If A:B=a:b, A/(A+B)=a/(a+b). A/B=a/b compares two parts.
- **Cross-multiplication:** a/b=c/d implies ad=bc for nonzero b and d.
- **Direct and inverse variation:** y=kx and xy=k respectively. Crew size and time are inverse for a fixed workload and equal output per worker.
- **Linked ratios:** Match the number of units of the shared quantity before combining.

## How to study it for Station Master

- Write each ratio in words, including which moment it describes. Mark the common entry in a linked ratio.
- For transfers write initial amounts as multiples, then apply both changes. For mixed variation keep a concrete output unit, such as metres per worker-day.

## Question models and worked examples

### Linked ratios

**Question.** A:B=3:4 and B:C=6:5. Split ₹1,860 between them.

1. Make B=12 in both versions: A:B=9:12 and B:C=12:10.
2. There are 31 parts worth 1860/31=60 rupees each.

**Answer:** ₹540, ₹720, ₹600.

### Ratio after a transfer

**Question.** Stocks are in ratio 7:5. Transfer 24 units from the first to the second and they become equal. Find the originals.

1. Set the amounts to 7k and 5k.
2. Now 7k−24=5k+24, so 2k=48. The gap closed by twice the transfer.

**Answer:** 168 and 120.

### Inverse and direct variation

**Question.** Eight workers mark 120 m in six days. How many days for 12 workers to mark 210 m at the same output per worker?

1. Initial output per worker-day is 120/(8×6)=2.5 m.
2. New job requires 210/2.5=84 worker-days. Divide by 12.

**Answer:** 7 days.

### Weighted mixture

**Question.** Mix 30% and 50% solutions in a 3:2 mass ratio. Find the resulting concentration.

1. Use 3 and 2 mass units. Solute is 0.30×3+0.50×2=1.9 units.
2. Divide solute 1.9 by mixture mass 5.

**Answer:** 38%.

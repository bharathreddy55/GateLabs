## What it means

A work rate is the fraction of a task done per unit time. Under a constant-rate model, workers operating together contribute work simultaneously. Partial work done before a change of crew or an interruption remains complete. A leak or drain reduces progress and has a negative rate.

## Formulas and rules

- **One worker:** If A takes a days alone, A does 1/a of the job each day. Work=rate×time.
- **Team:** Add signed rates. Divide remaining work by a positive net rate to find the finish time.
- **Efficiency:** For equal jobs, completion times are inverse to efficiency. A 3:2 efficiency ratio means a 2:3 time ratio.
- **LCM method:** Use a total number of work units divisible by each individual completion time, so daily outputs can be integers.

## How to study it for Station Master

- Use a timeline whenever someone joins, leaves or alternates. Annotate each phase with rate, duration and output.
- Practise finishing partway through a final turn. In tank questions verify which taps add and which remove water.

## Question models and worked examples

### Worker leaves

**Question.** A needs 12 days alone and B needs 18. They work together four days before A leaves. When is the job done?

1. Use 36 units: A produces 3/day and B 2/day. Four days give 20 units.
2. B finishes the remaining 16 units in eight more days.

**Answer:** 12 days in total.

### Tank and leak

**Question.** A tap fills a tank in eight hours and a leak empties it in 24 hours. How long from empty with both open?

1. On a 24-unit scale the tap contributes +3/hour and the leak −1/hour.
2. Net rate=2/hour. Divide 24 by 2.

**Answer:** 12 hours.

### Alternating work

**Question.** A and B can complete a job in six and eight days respectively. They alternate days starting with A. Find finish time.

1. Use 24 units. A does 4 per day and B does 3. Each full pair of days supplies 7 units.
2. Three pairs produce 21 units. The remaining 3 take A 3/4 of the next day.

**Answer:** 6¾ days.

### Recover a worker’s rate

**Question.** Together A and B take 10 days. A alone takes 15. How long for B alone?

1. Subtract A’s rate: 1/10−1/15=1/30 job per day.
2. Invert the rate to get time for a whole job.

**Answer:** 30 days.

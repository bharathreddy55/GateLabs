A linear equation in one variable reduces to ax+b=0. Preserve equality by performing the same operations on both sides. For two unknowns, substitute one expression into the other equation or eliminate a variable by adding scaled equations.

## Concepts in depth

Translate each total or difference into a separate equation. Elimination combines equations to remove one unknown. Substitution expresses one variable in terms of the other. A context may require integers or nonnegative quantities even when the algebra yields a real solution.

## How to apply it

For 70 tickets costing ₹2,980 at ₹50 or ₹30, suppose all 70 cost ₹30. The extra ₹880 must be ₹20 per adult ticket, giving 44 adult tickets. As a check, the other 26 tickets complete the total and the revenue.

## Worked example

Solve 3x−4=11. Add 4 to both sides to get 3x=15, then divide by 3 to get x=5.

## Common mistake

Multiplying only one term of an equation changes the problem. Apply the operation to the entire side.

## Practice

Given x+y=12 and x−y=4, find x and y.

**Answer:** Add to get 2x=16, so x=8 and y=4.

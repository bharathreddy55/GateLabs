## What it means

On a standard analogue clock, the minute hand makes a full turn per hour and the hour hand makes one in twelve hours. Both hands move continuously. Measure positions clockwise from 12 and compare their angular separation. A wording such as “smaller angle” chooses the shorter of two arcs.

## Formulas and rules

- **Positions:** At h hours and m minutes on a twelve-hour clock, minute hand=6m° and hour hand=30h+0.5m°. Use h=0 for hour 12.
- **Angle:** Set d to the absolute difference of positions. Smaller angle=min(d,360°−d).
- **Relative rate:** The minute hand gains 6−0.5=5.5° per minute on the hour hand. Successive coincidences are 360/5.5=720/11 minutes apart.
- **Target positions:** Coincidence means separation 0 modulo 360°, opposition 180°, and right angles ±90° modulo 360°.

## How to study it for Station Master

- Draw the 12, 3, 6 and 9 anchors and estimate the requested arc before solving. Keep the hour hand’s 0.5° per minute in every example.
- Drill an angle at a known time and the reverse with two possible answers. After solving for m, keep times only in the requested hour.

## Question models and worked examples

### Angle at a stated time

**Question.** What is the smaller angle at 8:20?

1. Hour position=240°+10°=250°. Minute position=120°.
2. Absolute difference=130°. The other arc is 230°.

**Answer:** 130°.

### Coincidence

**Question.** When between 2:00 and 3:00 do the hands overlap?

1. At 2:00 the hour hand is 60° ahead. The minute hand gains 5.5° per minute.
2. Time is 60/5.5=120/11 minutes after 2:00, about 10 minutes 54.5 seconds.

**Answer:** About 2:10:54.5.

### Right angle with two solutions

**Question.** When between 4:00 and 5:00 do the hands make right angles?

1. The signed hour-minus-minute separation is 120−5.5m degrees. Set it to +90°: m=30/5.5=60/11 minutes.
2. Set it to −90°: m=210/5.5=420/11 minutes. Both lie in the given hour.

**Answer:** About 4:05:27.3 and 4:38:10.9.

### Smaller and larger arc

**Question.** Find both angles at 11:10.

1. Hour position=330°+5°=335°, minute position=60°.
2. The clockwise gap has magnitude 275°. Its complementary full-circle arc is 85°.

**Answer:** 85° smaller, 275° larger.

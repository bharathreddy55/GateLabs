The minute hand moves 6° per minute, and the hour hand moves 0.5° per minute. At h:m, take the difference between 30h+0.5m and 6m. For the smaller angle, compare the absolute difference with its complement to 360°.

## Concepts in depth

From 12 o'clock, the minute hand position is 6m degrees and the hour hand is 30h+0.5m degrees. Use the smaller of the absolute difference and its complement to 360°. Relative angular speed is 5.5 degrees per minute.

## How to apply it

At 4:40 the hands are at 140° and 240°, giving a 100° gap. The hour hand does not remain fixed at the four. For coincidences, start from the initial lead and divide the required gain by 5.5.

## Worked example

At 3:20, the hour hand is at 100° and minute hand at 120°. Their smaller separation is 20°.

## Common mistake

The hour hand continues moving between whole hours.

## Practice

Find the smaller angle at 6:00.

**Answer:** 180°, because the hands point to 6 and 12.

The Seventh Schedule groups legislative fields into Union, State and Concurrent Lists. Article 246 and related provisions distribute legislative competence, subject to constitutional exceptions. Administrative relations concern implementation, coordination and directions where authorised. Fiscal relations include assignments of taxes, transfers and grants. The Finance Commission under Article 280 recommends the distribution of specified revenues and grants.

Distinguish constitutionally established bodies such as the Election Commission and CAG from statutory bodies such as the National Human Rights Commission and information commissions. The Right to Information Act provides access to covered public information under stated procedures and exemptions. The Lokpal deals with specified Union-level public functionaries under statute. State Lokayukta powers depend on the applicable law.

## Worked example

For a question about vertical tax devolution between Union and states, pick the Finance Commission. For government audit reports, look for the CAG.

## Common mistake

A body's functions, constitutional or statutory basis, and jurisdiction are three different attributes.

## Practice

Which constitutional article provides for the Finance Commission?

**Answer:** Article 280 deals with the Finance Commission.

India uses a parliamentary executive. At Union level, the Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to the Lok Sabha. The President is constitutional head and generally acts on ministerial advice within the constitutional scheme. Parliament includes the President and its two Houses. States may have one legislative chamber or two under the relevant provisions. Compare legislative powers over ordinary bills and money bills before answering procedure questions.

Courts interpret the Constitution and laws, adjudicate disputes and enforce legal limits through judicial review. Cases can raise validity of legislation or executive action. Judicial activism describes a style or intensity of judicial intervention, often discussed alongside public interest litigation, and is distinct from the power of judicial review itself.

If a no-confidence motion succeeds in the Lok Sabha, identify the collective responsibility of the Council of Ministers as the governing principle.

## Common mistake

A bicameral legislature does not necessarily give both houses equal authority on every bill.

## Practice

The Union Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to which body?

**Answer:** Article 75(3) makes the Council of Ministers collectively responsible to the House of the People.

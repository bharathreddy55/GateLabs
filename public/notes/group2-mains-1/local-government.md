The Community Development Programme sought to support local rural development. The Balwant Rai Mehta Committee recommended democratic decentralisation through a three-tier Panchayati Raj model. The Ashok Mehta Committee proposed a two-tier model centred on district and mandal levels. Read the reports in their policy context, then compare their proposals with the later constitutional arrangements.

The 73rd Amendment inserted Part IX and the Eleventh Schedule for Panchayats. The 74th Amendment inserted Part IXA and the Twelfth Schedule for Municipalities. Study the Gram Sabha, election and reservation provisions, State Election Commissions and State Finance Commissions. Effective decentralisation depends on state laws and actual transfer of funds, functions and staff.

## Concepts in depth

Compare Panchayats under Part IX and Municipalities under Part IXA. The Eleventh and Twelfth Schedules list subject areas, but actual devolution depends on state legislation and practice. Study elections, reservations, duration, financial review and local planning. Committees such as Balwant Rai Mehta and Ashok Mehta preceded constitutional changes.

## How to apply it

The State Election Commission and State Finance Commission have different mandates. A function listed in a schedule may still lack funds, authority or personnel. For a reform question distinguish a directly elected institution from an administrative programme such as the Community Development Programme.

## Worked example

A water or sanitation function can appear in a schedule. To determine who is responsible in practice, also check how the state assigned and funded that function.

## Common mistake

A subject listed in a schedule does not by itself establish the same implementation in every state.

## Practice

Which schedule lists subjects associated with Panchayats?

**Answer:** The Eleventh Schedule is associated with rural Panchayats under Part IX.

Trace the overlapping rule of regional houses, including the Velanati Chodas, Kakatiyas, Reddis and Vijayanagara, using inscriptions and dated monuments. Kakatiya expansion centred on Warangal and drew on agricultural settlement, irrigation and long-distance trade. Study the Motupalli charter as evidence of concern for maritime merchants and trade. After the Kakatiya state, regional powers such as the Reddis and Recherla chiefs shaped inland and coastal politics. Vijayanagara links the Andhra region to wider south Indian political, commercial and artistic networks.

Take separate notes on irrigation tanks, temples, merchant networks, social groups, patronage and religious practice. The Ramappa temple is associated with Kakatiya architecture and Lepakshi's Veerabhadra temple with the Vijayanagara period. Telugu literature connects several periods, including Tikkana's Mahabharata work, Srinatha's courtly literature and Krishnadevaraya's Amuktamalyada. Compare chronology, patron, language and genre in matching questions.

## Worked example

A question pairs maritime trade with Motupalli and patronage with a royal charter. Link these clues to the Kakatiya polity and coastal commerce.

## Common mistake

Avoid assuming dynasties succeeded one another uniformly across the whole region. Their territories overlapped.

## Practice

Which ruler authored the Telugu work Amuktamalyada?

**Answer:** Amuktamalyada is associated with Krishnadevaraya of Vijayanagara.

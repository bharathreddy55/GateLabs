Federal features include constitutional division of powers and representation of states in the Rajya Sabha. Unitary features include strong Union powers in specified situations and central roles under emergency provisions. Compare the Rajamannar Committee on centre–state relations with the Sarkaria and Punchhi commissions. Separate their recommendations from the constitutional provisions that were actually implemented.

The Election Commission administers elections within its jurisdiction and recognises national and state parties under the Election Symbols Order. Recognition criteria and current party status can change. Distinguish registration, recognition and allotment of symbols. Electoral reforms address campaign finance, disclosure, administration and participation. The Tenth Schedule addresses disqualification on grounds of defection, with specified exceptions and decision procedures.

## Worked example

A question cites voting against a party direction and asks which constitutional schedule governs possible disqualification. Identify the Tenth Schedule and check the facts against its conditions.

## Common mistake

Recognition as a state or national party is different from registration as a political party.

## Practice

Which schedule contains the Constitution's anti-defection provisions?

**Answer:** The Tenth Schedule contains the disqualification rules for defection.

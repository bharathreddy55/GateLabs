European merchants used textile and maritime centres such as Masulipatnam. Company control of the Northern Circars and its revenue administration changed regional politics, links between cultivators and intermediaries, and access to forest resources. Read the impact of 1857 by locality and distinguish it from later resistance such as the Rampa uprising of 1922–24 led by Alluri Sitarama Raju.

Social reformers including Kandukuri Veeresalingam used education, publications and associations in campaigns concerning women's education and widow remarriage. Compare the non-Brahmin Justice Party in the Madras Presidency with the Self-Respect movement associated with E. V. Ramasamy. Track Gandhian campaigns, local participation such as Chirala–Perala, socialist and communist organisation, kisan and anti-zamindari activism, and the local grievances behind each. Nationalist songs, revolutionary writing and theatre circulated ideas beyond formal meetings. Study women as organisers, writers and participants, including Durgabai Deshmukh's public mobilisation and institution-building.

## Worked example

Given a forest grievance, tribal mobilisation and the years 1922–24, identify the Rampa uprising. A different question about Chirala–Perala asks about a civic and tax protest.

## Common mistake

Chronology matters. Reform associations, peasant mobilisation and the Congress campaigns often overlapped, with different aims.

## Practice

Which reformer is closely associated with widow remarriage advocacy in Andhra?

**Answer:** Veeresalingam campaigned for widow remarriage and women's education in the Telugu-speaking region.

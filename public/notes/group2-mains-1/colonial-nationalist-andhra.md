## Concepts in depth

Track coastal factories and Company territorial power separately. The Northern Circars connect European rivalry with the transition from commercial influence to territorial revenue. Reformers used print, schools and associations to debate widow remarriage and women's education. Later Congress, peasant, anti-zamindari and communist activity had different organisational bases.

## How to apply it

Use a three-column chronology separating colonial institutions, reform campaigns and nationalist mobilisation. Associate Kandukuri Veeresalingam with reform and Tanguturi Prakasam with nationalist and political leadership. Avoid giving a single cause or social constituency to all movements. Explain how literature, theatre and newspapers could circulate political ideas independently of formal party decisions.

## Worked example

Given a forest grievance, tribal mobilisation and the years 1922–24, identify the Rampa uprising. A different question about Chirala–Perala asks about a civic and tax protest.

## Common mistake

Chronology matters. Reform associations, peasant mobilisation and the Congress campaigns often overlapped, with different aims.

The demand for a separate Andhra province developed around language, political representation, educational institutions and administration in the Madras Presidency. Follow the Andhra conferences and Mahasabhas, Telugu newspapers, public associations and leaders such as Konda Venkatappayya and Tanguturi Prakasam. Libraries and reading rooms helped circulate ideas and build local organisation. Folk performance, songs and tribal traditions were important parts of a culturally diverse region and should be studied through specific communities, places and historical settings.

Potti Sriramulu's fast and death in 1952 accelerated the separate state demand. Parliament passed the Andhra State Act in 1953, and Andhra State was inaugurated on 1 October 1953 with Kurnool as its capital. Tanguturi Prakasam served as the first chief minister. Keep the Andhra movement in the Madras Presidency distinct from similarly named organisations and movements in the former Hyderabad State.

## Worked example

A chronology item places the fast and Andhra State Act next to Andhra Pradesh formation. Order them 1952, 1953 and 1956.

## Common mistake

The Andhra State created in 1953 and the Andhra Pradesh formed in 1956 were distinct territorial arrangements.

## Practice

Which city was the capital of Andhra State at its formation in 1953?

**Answer:** Kurnool was the capital of the Andhra State inaugurated in October 1953.

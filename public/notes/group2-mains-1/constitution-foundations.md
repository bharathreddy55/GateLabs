Trace the shift from colonial constitutional statutes to the Constituent Assembly and the Constitution that commenced on 26 January 1950. The Preamble states constitutional purposes and values. Part III protects fundamental rights, Part IV contains directive principles and Part IVA sets out fundamental duties. Remedies under Article 32 help enforce fundamental rights. Directive principles guide governance and are framed differently from enforceable rights.

Article 368 provides the principal amendment procedure. Study which changes need a special parliamentary majority and when ratification by at least half the state legislatures is required. Kesavananda Bharati established that the amendment power is subject to the basic structure doctrine. Compare constitutional supremacy, rule of law, judicial review, democratic government and federal features, which are central in basic structure discussions.

## Concepts in depth

Build chronology from Company and Crown statutes to the Government of India Act 1935, Constituent Assembly deliberation, adoption in November 1949 and commencement in January 1950. Fundamental rights restrict or guide State action according to their text. Directive Principles inform governance. Fundamental duties are in a separate part. Kesavananda Bharati limits amendment through the basic structure doctrine.

## How to apply it

Compare Articles 32 and 226 by court and scope. Article 226 can apply for purposes beyond enforcing fundamental rights. Some Article 368 amendments need ratification by at least half the state legislatures, depending on subject. An ordinary statutory amendment, a constitutional amendment and a judicial interpretation accomplish different institutional tasks.

## Worked example

A question asks about protecting Part III rights through the Supreme Court. Identify Article 32 and distinguish Article 226, which gives High Courts a wider writ jurisdiction.

## Common mistake

The Preamble and directive principles can inform interpretation. Do not equate every constitutional value with a directly enforceable individual claim.

## Practice

Which part contains the Directive Principles of State Policy?

**Answer:** Directive Principles appear in Part IV.

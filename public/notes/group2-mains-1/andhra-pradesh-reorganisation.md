Visalandhra advocates pressed for bringing Telugu-speaking regions together. The States Reorganisation Commission considered linguistic affinity alongside administrative, fiscal and regional concerns. It advised caution about an immediate merger of Telangana with Andhra. Leaders reached the 1956 Gentlemen’s Agreement to address safeguards such as regional development and employment concerns. The merger formed Andhra Pradesh on 1 November 1956 with Hyderabad as the capital.

Study subsequent social and cultural change through education, irrigation and regional inequality, language and cultural institutions, regional mobilisation in 1969 and 1972, subsequent accommodation efforts, and the movement for Telangana statehood. Under the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, Telangana became a separate state on 2 June 2014. Compare underlying regional concerns with the distinct formal instruments that implemented each territorial change.

## Concepts in depth

The States Reorganisation Commission considered language alongside administrative and economic factors. The 1956 merger combined Andhra State and Telangana, with the Gentlemen's Agreement addressing safeguards. Regional employment, expenditure and political representation remained significant. The Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act 2014 provided for the later bifurcation.

## How to apply it

Use a cause–decision–change timeline. Separate the SRC recommendation from the final political decision and label each safeguard by its intended purpose. Do not confuse a political agreement, statutory provisions and subsequent implementation. For post-1956 developments, consider regional differences in schooling, urban growth, political movements and cultural production.

## Worked example

If a question asks which instrument framed safeguards ahead of the 1956 merger, use the Gentlemen’s Agreement. A question on the 2014 state division points to the Reorganisation Act.

## Common mistake

A commission report is advisory. Identify the statute or formal decision that implemented a boundary change.

## Practice

On what date did Andhra Pradesh come into being in the 1956 reorganisation?

**Answer:** The merger of Andhra State and Telangana took effect on 1 November 1956.

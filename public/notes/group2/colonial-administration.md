British rule reshaped land revenue, law and education over time. Under the Permanent Settlement in Bengal, the government's assessed demand from zamindars was fixed. Ryotwari arrangements in much of the Madras and Bombay Presidencies dealt directly with cultivators. Mahalwari assessment operated through village estates or groups in parts of northern India. Each system affected who bore assessment and collection obligations.

Railways, telegraph lines and postal expansion increased the movement of goods and information and served colonial military and commercial priorities. English education expanded alongside vernacular print, later providing forums for public debate and political organization. The Indian Penal Code was enacted in 1860. The growth of cities and new professions altered class formation, while exposure to markets and taxation affected cultivators and artisans unevenly.

## Concepts in depth

Permanent Settlement treated zamindars as revenue intermediaries with a fixed demand in its defining arrangement. Ryotwari dealt with cultivators individually. Mahalwari used an estate or village basis. Colonial education, print and law created new institutions without affecting populations uniformly.

## How to apply it

In a matching question identify who contracted with the state, what unit assessment used, and whether revision was contemplated. Compare official goals with effects on cultivators, indebtedness and intermediaries instead of assuming one settlement covered all India.

## Worked example

A question describes an assessment settled directly with cultivators in areas under the Madras Presidency. The identifying feature is the revenue relationship with the ryot, so choose Ryotwari.

## Common mistake

Permanent Settlement fixed the state demand on the zamindar. Cultivators' rents could still change.

## Practice

Why did railways matter to both administrators and nationalists?

**Answer:** Administrators moved troops and commodities more readily. Political organizers and newspapers also reached wider audiences through interconnected regions.

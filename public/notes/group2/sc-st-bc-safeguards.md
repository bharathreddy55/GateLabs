Article 46 directs promotion of the educational and economic interests of weaker sections, in particular Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. Articles 341 and 342 concern specification of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. Articles 338, 338A and 338B concern the national commissions for SCs, STs and backward classes respectively. Reservation enabling provisions appear in Articles 15 and 16, with details dependent on the category and setting. Articles 330 and 332 provide for SC/ST seat reservations in the Lok Sabha and state assemblies.

## Worked example

A question asks for the constitutional article for the National Commission for Scheduled Tribes. Choose Article 338A. Article 338 concerns the commission for SCs.

## Common mistake

Separate the list of recognized communities, the commission that monitors safeguards and the specific reservation rule being tested.

## Practice

Which provision is a Directive Principle emphasizing education and economic interests of weaker sections?

**Answer:** Article 46.

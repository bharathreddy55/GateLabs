Exam topics include domestic violence, dowry-related abuse, sexual harassment, trafficking and other forms of gender-based violence. The Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act provides remedies that can include protection, residence and monetary relief. The 2013 workplace sexual harassment law provides for prevention and complaint mechanisms. Study statutory terminology and reporting data alongside social factors such as dependency and barriers to seeking support.

## Concepts in depth

Study physical, sexual, economic and psychological forms of violence as distinct but sometimes overlapping categories. The Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act recognises several forms of domestic abuse and provides a framework for remedies. Workplace harassment has a separate statutory framework.

## How to apply it

Recorded crimes, reported experiences and actual prevalence are different measures. A rise in reporting can reflect better access or awareness as well as a change in underlying violence. For matching questions identify whether the institution is a complaint body, protection mechanism or criminal justice agency.

## Worked example

If an item concerns workplace harassment, identify the workplace statute and complaints mechanism. If it concerns a residence order following domestic abuse, consider the Domestic Violence Act.

## Common mistake

Reported cases are affected by access to reporting, legal definitions and recording practice. They cannot by themselves measure incidence.

## Practice

Why can recorded cases rise after reporting access improves?

**Answer:** People previously unable or unwilling to report may gain a practical route to do so.

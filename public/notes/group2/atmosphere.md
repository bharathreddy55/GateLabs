Dry air near sea level is about 78% nitrogen and 21% oxygen by volume, with argon and other gases making up most of the remainder. Water vapour varies strongly with location and altitude. Greenhouse gases absorb and emit infrared radiation, influencing the surface energy budget.

Temperature generally falls upward through the troposphere, where most weather occurs. In the stratosphere, absorption of ultraviolet radiation by ozone helps temperature rise with height. Temperature generally falls through the mesosphere and rises through the thermosphere as dilute gases absorb energetic solar radiation. Layer boundaries vary with latitude, season and solar activity. Pressure falls with altitude as the mass of overlying air decreases.

## Worked example

A profile shows falling temperature up to the tropopause and then a warming trend. The inversion above the boundary identifies the stratosphere, where ozone absorption heats the air.

## Common mistake

Distinguish stratospheric ozone's ultraviolet shielding from the role of greenhouse gases in absorbing outgoing infrared radiation.

## Practice

Why does most cloud formation occur in the troposphere?

**Answer:** This layer contains most atmospheric water vapour, and rising air can cool to saturation there.

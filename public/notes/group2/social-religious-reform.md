Reform developed through religious debate, schools, associations, writing and campaigns over law. Raja Rammohun Roy is associated with the Brahmo Sabha and public campaigning against sati. Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar promoted widow remarriage, legalized by the Hindu Widows' Remarriage Act of 1856. Dayanand Saraswati founded the Arya Samaj in 1875 and called for a return to Vedic authority. Syed Ahmad Khan encouraged modern education through the Aligarh movement.

Jyotirao and Savitribai Phule advanced girls' education and challenged caste hierarchy. The Satyashodhak Samaj, founded by Jyotirao in 1873, promoted equality and challenged Brahmanical dominance. Kandukuri Veeresalingam advocated women's education and widow remarriage in Andhra. In the twentieth century, B. R. Ambedkar organized for civil rights, education and political representation. The Mahad satyagraha of 1927 asserted equal access to public water.

## Concepts in depth

Reform movements differed by region, social constituencies and agenda. Brahmo reform is associated with Rammohun Roy, Arya Samaj with Dayananda Saraswati, and Satyashodhak Samaj with Jyotirao Phule. Women's education and critiques of entrenched hierarchies took many forms.

## How to apply it

Treat the date of legal reform, campaigning and actual social change separately. In Andhra, Kandukuri Veeresalingam is associated with widow remarriage and women's education. Avoid grouping all movements under an identical religious programme.

## Worked example

A question pairs 1873 with a challenge to caste oppression and ritual dominance in western India. Choose Satyashodhak Samaj. A Vedic reform association founded in 1875 is Arya Samaj.

## Common mistake

Identify the specific constituency and method of each movement. Education campaigns, caste resistance and religious reform sometimes overlapped.

## Practice

Who links Telugu public debate with campaigns for widow remarriage?

**Answer:** Kandukuri Veeresalingam, who used writing and reform associations to advance social change in Andhra.

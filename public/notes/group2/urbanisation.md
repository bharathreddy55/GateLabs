Urbanisation means a rising share of people living in urban settlements. It can result from net rural-to-urban migration, differences in natural increase, reclassification and incorporation of nearby settlements. Indian urban statistics distinguish statutory towns, officially designated as urban, from census towns identified by census criteria. Visakhapatnam and the Vijayawada–Guntur area illustrate different urban functions in Andhra Pradesh, including port industry, commerce, services and administration.

## Worked example

If the urban population rises from 20 to 22 million while the total rises from 50 to 60 million, the urban share falls from 40% to about 36.7%. Urban population grew, while the urbanisation share declined.

## Common mistake

A larger urban population and a larger urban share measure different outcomes.

## Practice

How can reclassification increase urbanisation without individuals changing homes?

**Answer:** Residents of a newly classified urban settlement enter the urban count at the same address.

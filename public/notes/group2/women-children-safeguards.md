Article 15(3) enables special provisions for women and children. Article 21A concerns free and compulsory education for children aged six to fourteen. Article 24 addresses hazardous employment below fourteen. Directive Principles include protection of children and provision for maternity relief. Articles 243D and 243T require women's reservation in panchayats and municipalities within their terms. Statutes and programmes support education, nutrition, workplace safety and child protection.

## Concepts in depth

Article 15(3) enables special provisions for women and children. Article 21A concerns free and compulsory education for ages six to fourteen, while Article 24 restricts child employment in specified settings. Statutes and commissions address additional matters and use separate operational definitions.

## How to apply it

Match an education right, a labour restriction and a protection procedure separately. The age attached to a constitutional education right need not match the age used in a child sexual-offences statute. Learn authority and function for each institution.

## Worked example

A question asks which constitutional provision guarantees free and compulsory education to the specified age band. Match six to fourteen to Article 21A, then distinguish it from the employment rule in Article 24.

## Common mistake

A constitutional enabling provision, a fundamental right and a Directive Principle serve different functions. Match the verb used in the question.

## Practice

Which article allows special provisions for both women and children?

**Answer:** Article 15(3).

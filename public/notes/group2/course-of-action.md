A suitable course of action addresses the cause or consequence in the statement, is feasible and is proportionate. Urgent and preventive actions can both follow if each has a direct connection to the problem.

## Concepts in depth

A course of action should address the actual problem by a workable mechanism. Immediate containment can sit alongside investigation or prevention. Check which authority could act and how the proposed step follows from what is known.

## How to apply it

If a bridge report identifies one unsafe segment, inspecting and securing that segment while assessing adjacent areas directly addresses the evidence. Punishing unrelated users is neither targeted nor a remedy. Avoid extreme options that assume facts not given.

## Worked example

A contaminated water sample is confirmed in one supply zone. Issuing a targeted warning and investigating the source directly address the immediate and continuing risks.

## Common mistake

Beware of region-wide permanent measures based on a single localized problem when narrower measures are available.

## Practice

A bridge inspection finds a dangerous crack. Which comes first, closing access pending review or redesigning the entire city's transport system?

**Answer:** Close or restrict access pending technical review. It tackles the immediate risk with a feasible action.

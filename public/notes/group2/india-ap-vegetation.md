India's major vegetation formations include tropical evergreen, moist and dry deciduous, thorn, montane and littoral or swamp communities. Rainfall amount and seasonality, elevation, soil and disturbance help determine distribution. Evergreen forests are associated with very wet regions, while thorn scrub occurs in drier environments. Montane vegetation varies with elevation. Mangroves occupy intertidal environments where salinity and flooding impose strong constraints.

Much of the Andhra Pradesh interior and Eastern Ghats supports deciduous forest and scrub, with moister vegetation in suitable hill tracts. Red sanders is associated with parts of the southern Eastern Ghats. The Coringa mangroves near Kakinada occupy tidal creeks in the Godavari delta region. Mangroves provide habitat, retain sediment and can reduce wave energy where the forest and coastal conditions permit.

## Concepts in depth

Evergreen formations favour persistently moist conditions, deciduous forests respond to seasonality, and thorn or scrub vegetation can occur in drier areas. Mangroves occupy suitable intertidal settings combining saline water and sediment.

## How to apply it

Locate mangroves in deltaic coastal settings such as the Godavari estuary. Eastern Ghats forest types vary with rainfall, elevation and disturbance. A protected area label does not specify one vegetation type throughout.

## Worked example

A question combines salt-tolerant vegetation with tidal creeks near Kakinada. Match it to the Coringa mangroves. An inland dry hill range calls for a different vegetation community.

## Common mistake

Forest cover reported from imagery and the extent legally designated as forest measure different things. Use the stated definition when comparing statistics.

## Practice

Why are mangroves geographically restricted?

**Answer:** They are adapted to intertidal environments where salinity, waterlogging and recurring tidal inundation shape plant growth.

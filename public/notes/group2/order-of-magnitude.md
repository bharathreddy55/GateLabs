Write numbers in scientific notation to compare scale. One order of magnitude corresponds to a factor of ten. When asked for the nearest power of ten on a logarithmic scale, compare the leading coefficient to the square root of ten, about 3.16. Use the convention stated in the question.

## Concepts in depth

Represent a positive quantity as a×10ⁿ with 1≤a<10. Compare the powers first, then the mantissas. When questions say closest power of ten, be mindful of whether the question specifies logarithmic scale or ordinary absolute distance.

## How to apply it

A rectangle of about 2.9 m by 0.041 m has area about 0.12 m², near 10⁻¹ m². Convert before comparing areas. If answer choices differ only slightly, use bounds or exact calculation rather than successive rough rounding.

## Worked example

Compare 8,000 and 80,000. Their ratio is 10, so the latter is one order of magnitude larger.

## Common mistake

Retain units before estimating. Convert metres and kilometres to a common unit first.

## Practice

A quantity rises from 0.002 to 0.2. How many orders of magnitude is the increase?

**Answer:** Two. The ratio is 100, or 10².

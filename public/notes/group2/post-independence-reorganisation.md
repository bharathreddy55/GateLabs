At independence, British paramountcy over princely states lapsed. Vallabhbhai Patel and V. P. Menon helped negotiate accession and integration. Junagadh, Hyderabad and Jammu and Kashmir followed different paths. Hyderabad was incorporated after Operation Polo in 1948. The Constitution took effect on 26 January 1950 and provided a framework for the Union and states.

After Potti Sriramulu's fast and death in 1952 and the ensuing movement, Andhra State was created from Madras State on 1 October 1953. The States Reorganisation Commission examined language alongside administrative and economic considerations. The States Reorganisation Act of 1956 reshaped boundaries. Andhra State merged with the Telangana region of Hyderabad State to form Andhra Pradesh on 1 November 1956. Under the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, Telangana became a separate state on 2 June 2014.

## Concepts in depth

Accession and integration of princely states differ from later reorganisation of state boundaries. Andhra State came into being in 1953, while States Reorganisation in 1956 combined Andhra State with Telangana to form Andhra Pradesh. Telangana became a separate state in 2014.

## How to apply it

Place the States Reorganisation Commission's assessment before the 1956 changes. Distinguish the capitals Kurnool for Andhra State and Hyderabad in the combined state. In a polity question, Articles 2 and 3 deal with related but distinct parliamentary actions.

## Worked example

A question asks whether Andhra Pradesh was formed in 1953 or 1956. Andhra State was created in 1953. Andhra Pradesh followed when Telangana joined it in 1956.

## Common mistake

Separate princely-state accession, linguistic reorganisation and the 2014 bifurcation. Each involved different institutions and dates.

## Practice

Which law created the separate state of Telangana?

**Answer:** The Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014. The appointed day was 2 June 2014.

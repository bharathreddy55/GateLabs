A conclusion follows when it is supported by the statements under the reasoning standard in the question. In deduction, try to construct a case in which the premises hold and the conclusion fails. In a passage, look for explicit scope and qualifiers.

## Concepts in depth

Read conclusions against the literal statement. A possibility, prediction or recommendation may need additional evidence. For numerical conclusions confirm baseline and denominator. For categorical statements track quantifiers such as some and all.

## How to apply it

“Most respondents preferred online forms” supports that preference within the respondent group. It does not imply every applicant prefers them. If an option is false under one plausible situation consistent with the statement, it is not a necessary conclusion.

## Worked example

Some employees work remotely. Every remote worker has secure access. At least some employees have secure access, because the remote workers provide examples.

## Common mistake

Preserve quantifiers. Some does not imply most, and an association alone gives no causal direction.

## Practice

No trainees hold permanent posts. Some technicians are trainees. Can some technicians be inferred to lack permanent posts?

**Answer:** Yes. The technicians who are trainees necessarily lack permanent posts.

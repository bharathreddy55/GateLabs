Induction generalizes from observations or patterns. Its strength depends on sample size, representativeness and alternatives. A larger biased sample can still produce a poor inference. Identify the population and the scope of the conclusion.

## Worked example

A survey at one coaching centre finds that most attendees prefer morning tests. It supports a conclusion about the surveyed group. A statewide claim needs a broader design.

## Common mistake

Words such as always and necessarily usually overstate what a sample supports.

## Practice

A factory samples units at random across all shifts and finds a defect rate near 2%. Is the next unit necessarily defective with frequency exactly 2% in every batch?

**Answer:** No. The sample estimates a population rate. Individual outcomes and future batches can vary.

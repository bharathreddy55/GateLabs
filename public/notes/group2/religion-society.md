Religion has personal and institutional dimensions, including beliefs, ritual, communities, festivals and organizations. Religious identities intersect with caste, gender, region and language. Sociologists examine how institutions create solidarity and also how social boundaries can become contested. Constitutional Articles 25–28 address religious freedom and related questions, subject to their specific terms.

## Worked example

A survey records religious affiliation, and a separate survey records frequency of worship. The first measures identity or affiliation. The second measures one aspect of practice.

## Common mistake

Avoid inferring an individual's beliefs or practice from community membership alone.

## Practice

What makes religion a social institution?

**Answer:** It can organize recurring roles, norms, rituals and collective resources across a community.

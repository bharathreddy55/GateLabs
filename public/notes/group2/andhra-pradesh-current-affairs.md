Coverage: selected Andhra Pradesh event, 12–13 November 2026. Information checked on 18 November 2026.

Visakhapatnam hosted the 31st CII Partnership Summit. The Union Commerce and Industry Minister addressed the inaugural session on 12 November. The Vice-President addressed the valedictory session on 13 November. The event discussed investment partnerships and Andhra Pradesh's industrial opportunities. In remarks reported by PIB Vijayawada, the minister cited a green hydrogen hub at Pudimadaka, a drone city at Orvakal and a space city at Tirupati.

For APPSC, connect each location to its activity and place it on a state map. The named projects illustrate different location needs. Hydrogen production can depend on electricity, water, industrial customers and handling infrastructure. An aerospace or drone cluster can benefit from suppliers, testing facilities and skilled workers.

## Worked example

A matching question gives Pudimadaka, Orvakal and Tirupati. Pair them with green hydrogen, drones and space-related development respectively, using the locations named in the official address.

## Common mistake

Record separately whether an investment is a commitment, final approval, project under construction, or operational facility. A summit speech may report commitments at several stages.

## Practice

What city hosted the summit? **Answer:** Visakhapatnam. The inaugural and valedictory PIB releases both identify it as the venue.

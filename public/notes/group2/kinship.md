Kinship connects people through descent, marriage, adoption and other socially recognized ties. Consanguineal ties are understood through blood or descent, while affinal ties arise through marriage. Patrilineal descent traces membership through the father's line and matrilineal descent through the mother's. Residence, descent, inheritance and authority can follow different patterns in the same community.

## Worked example

In a matrilineal descent system, a child belongs to the mother's lineage. The child's household could still include a resident father, and authority depends on the specific institution.

## Common mistake

Distinguish matrilineal descent from matrilocal residence and from political authority held by women.

## Practice

What type of relative is a spouse's brother?

**Answer:** An affinal relative, because the relationship arises through marriage.

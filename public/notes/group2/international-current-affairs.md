Coverage: selected international event, 9–18 November 2026. Information checked on 18 November 2026.

The UN climate conference COP31 is scheduled for 9–20 November in Antalya, Türkiye. The opening of the first high-level segment took place on 11 November. The UNFCCC organizes the conference. COP refers to the Conference of the Parties to the climate convention. CMP refers to the meeting of parties to the Kyoto Protocol, and CMA to the meeting of parties to the Paris Agreement. Those bodies can meet during the same conference and adopt texts under separate legal frameworks.

The agenda connects emissions reduction, adaptation and finance. Mitigation reduces greenhouse-gas emissions or increases removals. Adaptation reduces harm from climate impacts. Finance can support either objective, depending on how funds are used.

## Worked example

A coastal early-warning system helps communities prepare for storm surge. Classify it as adaptation. Replacing a fossil generation source with lower-carbon energy is primarily mitigation.

## Common mistake

The hosts' programme and speeches indicate priorities and proposals. Use the relevant adopted decision text for claims about negotiated outcomes.

## Practice

Which meeting body deals with the Paris Agreement? **Answer:** The CMA. The reference to the Paris Agreement identifies the governing instrument.

Where is COP31 taking place? **Answer:** Antalya, Türkiye, according to the UNFCCC conference calendar.

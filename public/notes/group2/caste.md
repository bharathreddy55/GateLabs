Caste commonly refers to hereditary social groups with practices of endogamy, local status distinctions and historically linked occupations. Varna is a broad classificatory idea, while jatis are numerous communities whose local relationships cannot be inferred from a single fourfold model. Urban work, education, political organizations, law and migration can reshape caste practices and identities.

## Worked example

A question describes hundreds of named marriage communities with different local rankings. This points to jatis. A textbook scheme with four broad categories describes varna.

## Common mistake

Membership in an officially listed category requires the relevant legal list. A description of social status alone is insufficient.

## Practice

Why might a caste remain socially salient after occupational mobility increases?

**Answer:** Marriage networks, local association, status and political organization may preserve the identity after occupations diversify.

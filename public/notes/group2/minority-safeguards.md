Articles 25–28 address religious freedom and related matters. Article 29(1) protects the right of any section of citizens with a distinct language, script or culture to conserve it. Article 30(1) gives religious and linguistic minorities the right to establish and administer educational institutions of their choice. Article 350A concerns efforts to provide adequate mother-tongue instruction facilities at the primary stage for children of linguistic minorities. The National Commission for Minorities has a statutory basis.

## Worked example

An item asks about a linguistic minority establishing and administering an educational institution. Article 30(1) is the direct match. Preservation of a distinct script points to Article 29(1).

## Common mistake

Keep Article 29(1)'s wording in view. It refers to a section of citizens with a distinct language, script or culture.

## Practice

What two types of minorities are expressly named in Article 30(1)?

**Answer:** Minorities based on religion or language.

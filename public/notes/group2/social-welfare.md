Welfare measures include rights, public services, transfers, affirmative action and livelihood programmes. Delivery requires an identified eligible population, enrolment, funding and a way to resolve grievances. Across India and Andhra Pradesh, education scholarships, nutrition services and accessible public facilities address different disadvantages. Use the current government order or scheme guidelines for benefit amounts and eligibility.

## Concepts in depth

Separate social protection, service provision and capacity building. A programme's stated target, funding, administrator, delivery mechanism and measurable outcome answer different exam questions. Inputs and enrolments are more direct to observe than long-term wellbeing changes.

## How to apply it

A high allocation does not guarantee coverage if intended beneficiaries face documentation or access barriers. Evaluate inclusion errors, exclusion errors and actual benefits. In programme matching, date the version and identify Union or state responsibility.

## Worked example

A programme reports 10,000 enrolled beneficiaries but attendance and completion do not improve. Enrolment is an output. Attendance and completion are outcomes that need separate measurement.

## Common mistake

Allocated budgets and actual expenditures measure different stages. A large allocation alone cannot establish delivery.

## Practice

What is an exclusion error?

**Answer:** An intended eligible person is omitted or cannot access the programme.

Tribal or Adivasi communities have diverse languages, livelihoods and histories. Forest access, land rights, displacement, migration and interaction with markets have shaped particular communities differently. Scheduled Tribe is a constitutional administrative category notified for particular states or territories. The Fifth Schedule provides for the administration of Scheduled Areas and Scheduled Tribes in specified states. The Sixth Schedule has a distinct territorial scope in parts of the Northeast.

## Worked example

A question asks about governance in a notified Scheduled Area of Andhra Pradesh. Start with Fifth Schedule arrangements and the applicable law, while Sixth Schedule autonomous councils belong to specified northeastern areas.

## Common mistake

Avoid assuming all members of a Scheduled Tribe live in Scheduled Areas or have the same occupation.

## Practice

What distinguishes a tribal community from a Scheduled Area?

**Answer:** The first describes a community, while a Scheduled Area is a specifically notified territory.

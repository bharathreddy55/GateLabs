Industrial concentration reflects access to materials, power, labour, markets, capital and transport. India's iron and steel centres illustrate the value of mineral and rail connections. Cotton textiles historically clustered in western India, while new industrial corridors draw on improved logistics. In Andhra Pradesh, Visakhapatnam shows the advantages of port access for steel, petroleum and related activities. Limestone supplies cement production in parts of the interior. Sri City, near the Tamil Nadu border, illustrates a manufacturing cluster linked to the Chennai region's market and logistics.

## Concepts in depth

Industrial location balances inputs, energy, labour, land, water, markets and transport. Clustering can provide supplier networks and skilled labour but also congestion and environmental costs. Regional corridors link multiple nodes rather than describing one industrial estate.

## How to apply it

Visakhapatnam illustrates port access and heavy industry links. An IT cluster can prioritise skill supply and connectivity over proximity to ore. Match industries to the dominant location factor in the question's context.

## Worked example

For a plant that imports bulky raw materials by sea and ships large volumes, a port-adjacent location can reduce inland handling. A limestone-dependent cement plant can instead reduce raw-material transport by locating near deposits.

## Common mistake

Separate the factors behind an original industrial location from the reasons firms keep clustering there. Supplier and labour networks can sustain a cluster after transport conditions change.

## Practice

What is an agglomeration advantage?

**Answer:** A firm can share labour pools, services, infrastructure and suppliers with other nearby firms.

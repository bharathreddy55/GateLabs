Trade connects places that differ in resources, production costs, skills and market demand. Internal trade operates among regions of a country, and foreign trade crosses national boundaries. Ports, inland freight links, storage and customs procedures shape trading routes. Andhra Pradesh's maritime geography supports outward shipments of products such as marine foods and inward shipments of industrial inputs. Product-specific data vary by period and may be attributed to a port or to the producing state.

## Worked example

If a chart gives exports of ₹120 crore and imports of ₹150 crore, the trade balance is exports minus imports, or −₹30 crore. Total trade is ₹270 crore.

## Common mistake

Goods passing through an Andhra Pradesh port may originate in a neighbouring state. Port throughput and the state's own exports use different geographic definitions.

## Practice

Why does cold-chain reliability affect seafood trade?

**Answer:** It helps preserve safety and quality throughout processing, storage and delivery.

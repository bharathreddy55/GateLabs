Postal services, broadcasting, mobile networks and the internet carry information across space. Fibre offers high capacity along fixed routes, while wireless networks extend service to mobile users and areas where individual wire connections are difficult. Coverage, affordability, device access, digital literacy and connection quality all shape whether households can use services. In Andhra Pradesh, communication links support agricultural market information, emergency warnings, education and port logistics.

## Concepts in depth

Communication includes physical and digital networks. Coverage indicates where infrastructure is present. Capacity, cost, reliability and user skills affect effective access. Satellite, wireless and fibre networks serve different transmission and coverage needs.

## How to apply it

An area with nominal mobile coverage can still have a divide if service is costly or unstable. Use indicators such as subscription versus unique users carefully, since multiple subscriptions can belong to one person.

## Worked example

For a district warning about a cyclone, broadcasting and mobile alerts can reach many people quickly. Local language announcements and officers contacting vulnerable settlements help reach people with weak connectivity.

## Common mistake

Mobile coverage on a map measures potential access. It does not establish reliable service or that households have suitable devices.

## Practice

What is one way communication reduces costs in agricultural markets?

**Answer:** Price and demand information lets farmers compare potential buyers before paying to move produce.

Youth unrest can arise from perceived gaps between education, employment, social expectations and actual opportunities. Peer networks, student organizations and media can convert isolated grievances into collective demands. Protests may request jobs, transparency, affordable education or political representation. Responses can include dialogue, grievance resolution and longer-term changes in opportunity.

## Worked example

Graduates protest repeated recruitment delays. Identify the immediate trigger as postponed exams and investigate broader issues such as labour-market access and trust in institutions.

## Common mistake

Avoid treating a whole age group as homogeneous. Different class, gender and regional contexts shape participation and demands.

## Practice

Why can a credible exam and recruitment calendar reduce unrest?

**Answer:** It reduces uncertainty and gives applicants information they can use to plan their time and careers.

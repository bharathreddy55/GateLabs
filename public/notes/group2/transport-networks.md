Roads offer flexible, door-to-door connections. Railways are well suited to bulk freight and long-distance passenger movement. Waterways and coastal shipping can move heavy goods efficiently where channels, terminals and schedules support them. Air transport serves time-sensitive travel and higher-value freight. Visakhapatnam, Gangavaram, Kakinada and Krishnapatnam illustrate Andhra Pradesh's maritime links. Rail and roads connect coastal terminals to agricultural and industrial hinterlands.

## Concepts in depth

Transport networks connect settlements and production with markets. Road links offer flexible last-mile service. Rail and waterways often handle bulk flows efficiently where routes support them. Ports link hinterlands to maritime trade. Connectivity and accessibility are different measures.

## How to apply it

A new road can shorten travel time without increasing rail capacity. Compare mode by load, speed, terrain and origin–destination pair. A highly connected hub can have poor access for nearby settlements if last-mile links are weak.

## Worked example

A shipment of bulk material arriving in Visakhapatnam may then travel inland by rail, with trucks handling the final delivery. Choose each mode by shipment volume, time, distance and terminal access.

## Common mistake

A large port's influence depends on its hinterland connections as well as its location on the coast.

Child abuse includes physical, emotional and sexual abuse and neglect. The Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act covers sexual offences against children under 18 and provides child-sensitive procedures. The Juvenile Justice Act provides mechanisms for children in need of care and protection, including Child Welfare Committees. Education and community services contribute to prevention and early support.

## Worked example

An item asks which law addresses sexual offences against a person aged 16. The age falls within POCSO's definition of a child.

## Common mistake

Child protection concerns several forms of harm. Match the legal framework to the facts, such as sexual abuse, labour or a need for care.

## Practice

What is one role of a Child Welfare Committee?

**Answer:** It deals with the care, protection and rehabilitation of children in need of care and protection under the Juvenile Justice framework.

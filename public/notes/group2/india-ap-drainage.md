A drainage basin collects runoff for a river and its tributaries. A watershed divides adjacent basins. Himalayan systems include the Indus, Ganga and Brahmaputra. Many peninsular rivers, including the Godavari and Krishna, drain east to the Bay of Bengal. The Narmada and Tapi flow west and form estuaries. Whether a river builds a delta depends on sediment delivery, coastal currents, waves, tides and basin conditions.

The Godavari crosses the Eastern Ghats before reaching the Andhra coastal plain and delta. The Krishna is joined by the Tungabhadra upstream and passes through the state toward its delta. The Penna, or Pennar, drains parts of Rayalaseema and Nellore. The Vamsadhara and Nagavali are distinct rivers in the north coastal region. Kolleru is a freshwater lake between the Krishna and Godavari deltas, while Pulicat is a coastal brackish lagoon on the Andhra Pradesh–Tamil Nadu border.

## Worked example

An exam item describes a lake between the Krishna and Godavari deltas. The location identifies Kolleru. A brackish lagoon shared with Tamil Nadu identifies Pulicat.

## Common mistake

A river's basin extends across all contributing land, including upstream parts in other states. A state map shows only part of a large basin.

## Practice

Which major tributary joins the Krishna and drains parts of the southern Deccan?

**Answer:** The Tungabhadra, formed upstream by the Tunga and Bhadra rivers.

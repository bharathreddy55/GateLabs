The Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 addresses equality, accessibility, education, employment and participation. A benchmark disability ordinarily requires at least 40% of a specified disability under the statutory definition and certification framework. The benchmark category matters for some reservation and entitlement provisions. Reasonable accommodation is an adjustment that helps someone exercise rights on an equal basis, subject to the statutory standard.

## Worked example

A university provides an accessible assessment format tailored to a candidate's disability. This illustrates reasonable accommodation. Providing accessible classrooms from the outset illustrates broader accessibility.

## Common mistake

Check whether an exam question asks about a general right or a specific benchmark disability provision. The relevant eligibility rules can differ.

## Practice

How does inclusive education differ from physical admission alone?

**Answer:** It addresses access to teaching, assessment, facilities and support so admitted students can participate meaningfully.

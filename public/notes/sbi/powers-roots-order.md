Evaluate grouping first, then exponents, then multiplication and division left to right, and addition and subtraction left to right. A square uses the same factor twice and a cube three times. A square root denotes the nonnegative root of a nonnegative input. For nonzero a, a⁻ⁿ=1/aⁿ. Scientific notation uses a coefficient from 1 up to 10 multiplied by an integer power of ten.

## Concepts in depth

Treat brackets and root bars as grouping. Multiplication and division share precedence, as do addition and subtraction. A minus outside a power differs from a negative base raised to that power. Principal square roots are nonnegative.

## How to apply it

−3²=−9, while (−3)²=9. The equation x²=9 has roots ±3, but √9=3. Evaluate 48 ÷ 6 × 2 left to right to get 16. Before removing parentheses, decide whether a sign belongs to the base or applies to the result. A negative exponent takes a reciprocal and requires a nonzero base.

## Worked example

In 24÷6×2, divide first from the left to get 4, then multiply by 2 to get 8. In contrast, 24÷(6×2)=2.

## Common mistake

The letter order in BODMAS does not make multiplication universally earlier than division. Operations of the same precedence are evaluated left to right in these expressions.

Comparatives compare entities, and superlatives locate one at an extreme within a group. Positive degree can express equality with as … as. Connectors signal addition, contrast, result, concession and sequence. Real-life language functions include requesting, apologizing, agreeing, offering and giving directions. Choose form by function, audience and register.

## Concepts in depth

Connectors signal whether the next idea contrasts, supports, explains, or follows from the previous one. Although introduces a subordinate concession. However links clauses or sentences as a transition. Comparison must use parallel items and a shared scale.

## How to apply it

“The fare in A is lower than that in B” compares fares. “The fare in A is lower than B” compares a fare with a place. When choosing a connector, decide the direction first. For example, “Although costs rose, demand held steady” contrasts a likely expectation with the actual result.

## Worked example

'Although it rained, we played' marks concession. 'It rained, so we stopped' marks result. The same event can appear in sentences with different logical links.

## Common mistake

A grammatically correct connector can still misrepresent the intended logic.

## Practice

In 'Could you pass the chart?', what is the ordinary communicative function?

**Answer:** A polite request, usually for an action rather than information about ability.

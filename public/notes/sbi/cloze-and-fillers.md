Read enough of the passage to identify topic and tone before filling gaps. For each blank check grammar, meaning, collocation and how the sentence connects to nearby sentences. Reread with the selected words inserted.

## Concepts in depth

Read the entire cloze once for broad meaning. For each blank, predict part of speech and whether the missing idea adds, contrasts or concludes. Many wrong choices fit the dictionary meaning but fail the established phrase or the passage's tense.

## How to apply it

“She complied __ the instruction” needs with. “He is interested __ learning” needs in. After filling several blanks, reread the whole paragraph because a pronoun, connector or time marker may depend on a neighbouring answer.

## Worked example

'The road was flooded. ___, the bus was diverted.' 'Therefore' expresses the resulting diversion, while 'however' would signal contrast.

## Common mistake

A word may fit grammatically but fail to express the intended relationship or usual collocation.

## Practice

Choose a natural verb in 'Please ___ attention to the instructions'.

**Answer:** Pay, because pay attention is the conventional collocation.

Replace symbols with their defined comparison signs before chaining relationships. Transitivity works along consistent directions. A strict inequality remains strict when combined with a weak inequality of the same direction. Diverging arrows may leave the endpoints unordered.

## Worked example

A ≥ B and B > C imply A > C. But A > B and C > B do not order A and C.

## Common mistake

Check the direction before chaining. A shared smaller or larger middle element does not order the endpoints.

## Practice

If P ≤ Q = R < S, is P < S certain?

**Answer:** Yes. P is no greater than R, and R is strictly below S.

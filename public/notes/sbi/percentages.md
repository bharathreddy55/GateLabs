Convert p% to p/100. A rise by p% multiplies by 1+p/100 and a fall multiplies by 1−p/100. For successive changes, multiply the factors. Percentage-point change subtracts percentage values directly.

## Worked example

A price increases by 20% and then falls by 20%. The multiplier is 1.20 × 0.80 = 0.96. The final price is 4% below the original.

## Common mistake

Identify the denominator that defines the base. From 20% to 25% is a 5-percentage-point rise and a 25% relative increase.

## Practice

₹720 is 80% of what original amount?

**Answer:** ₹900, since 720 ÷ 0.80 = 900.

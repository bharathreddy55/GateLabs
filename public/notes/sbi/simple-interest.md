Simple interest accrues on the original principal. With annual percentage rate R and time T in years, interest is P × R × T / 100. Amount equals principal plus interest.

## Concepts in depth

Simple interest grows linearly because each period earns interest on the original principal. With annual rate r as a decimal, I=Prt for t in years, and amount=P+I. For months, match the rate and time units first.

## How to apply it

₹4,800 at 12% p.a. for 15 months earns 4800×0.12×1.25=₹720. Equal changes in time give equal increments under simple interest. Backsolve principal from amount by dividing by 1+rt.

## Worked example

₹5,000 at 8% annually for 18 months earns 5,000 × 0.08 × 1.5 = ₹600, making the amount ₹5,600.

## Common mistake

Convert months to years for an annual rate before substituting.

## Practice

5%, because 800 ÷ (4,000 × 4) = 0.05.

**Answer:** 5%, because 800 divided by 4,000 × 4 is 0.05.

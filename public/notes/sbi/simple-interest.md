Simple interest accrues on the original principal. With annual percentage rate R and time T in years, interest is P × R × T / 100. Amount equals principal plus interest.

## Worked example

₹5,000 at 8% annually for 18 months earns 5,000 × 0.08 × 1.5 = ₹600, making the amount ₹5,600.

## Common mistake

Convert months to years for an annual rate before substituting.

## Practice

5%, because 800 ÷ (4,000 × 4) = 0.05.

**Answer:** 5%, because 800 divided by 4,000 × 4 is 0.05.

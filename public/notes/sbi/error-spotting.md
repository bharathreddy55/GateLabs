Read the whole sentence before choosing a segment. Check agreement, tense, pronoun reference, parallel structure, articles and prepositions. Preserve the intended meaning when substituting a correction.

## Concepts in depth

Scan one relationship at a time, such as subject and verb, time and aspect, pronoun and referent, noun and modifier, preposition and collocation. A long grammatical phrase can sit between the true subject and its verb. Parallel lists should use parallel grammatical forms.

## How to apply it

“The manager asked us to check the figures, update the chart, and submitting the file” breaks the pattern. Complete the infinitive series with “submit the file.” For sentence improvement, keep the original meaning unless the question calls for a different one.

## Worked example

In 'Each of the forms were signed', each is the singular head. Change were to was.

## Common mistake

Treat regional or stylistic variation separately from a genuine grammatical error. Use the convention the prompt specifies.

## Practice

Find the agreement error in 'The list of branches are ready'.

**Answer:** Use 'is ready' because list is the singular subject.

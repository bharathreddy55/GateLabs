Use an east–west and north–south coordinate grid. Update a person's facing separately from their position. Left and right turns depend on facing. Shortest straight-line distance follows Pythagoras when the net movements are perpendicular.

## Worked example

Walk 3 km east, then 4 km north. Displacement lies northeast and is 5 km long. The distance walked is 7 km.

## Common mistake

The shortest displacement and total path length answer different questions.

## Practice

Walk 6 m north and 6 m south. What is the final displacement?

**Answer:** Zero, because the equal opposite motions cancel.

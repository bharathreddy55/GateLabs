Bar charts compare categories through lengths. Inspect scale, units, category order and whether bars are grouped or stacked. A stacked segment's size is the difference between its upper and lower boundaries.

## Worked example

A stacked bar totals 80 and the lower segment reaches 50. Its upper segment contributes 30, or 37.5% of the total.

## Common mistake

A truncated axis can exaggerate visual differences. Read numeric ticks rather than comparing apparent heights alone.

## Practice

A grouped chart shows 40 for A and 50 for B. How much larger is B relative to A?

**Answer:** 25%, because the difference of 10 is one quarter of A's 40.

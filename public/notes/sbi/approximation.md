Respect order of operations and use exact fractions where they are easy. Round only as much as the question permits. Estimate the resulting range, then compare options before deciding if a more precise computation is needed.

## Concepts in depth

Use simple nearby values for estimation only when the options are separated enough. Multiplying rounded values can amplify error. When a subtraction involves nearly equal values, calculate the difference before rounding.

## How to apply it

For 49.8% of 398, estimate 50% of 400≈200. If options are 198 and 201, refine your estimate by calculating 0.498×398=198.204. Estimate magnitude before calculating to reject impossible decimal placements. For close options, keep an error bound or perform the final multiplication exactly.

## Worked example

For about 19.8% of 503, use 20% of 500 = 100. The actual value is near 99.6, close enough if options are well separated.

## Common mistake

Applying rough rounding to close answer options can flip the choice. Keep extra significant figures or calculate exactly.

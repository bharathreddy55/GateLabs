Compound interest uses the accumulated balance. For principal P, periodic rate r as a decimal and n compounding periods, amount is P(1+r)ⁿ. With a nominal annual rate and quarterly compounding, divide the rate by four and use four periods per year.

## Worked example

₹10,000 at 10% annually for two years becomes ₹12,100. The second year earns ₹1,100 because its starting balance is ₹11,000.

## Common mistake

Separate amount from interest. The interest in the example is ₹2,100.

## Practice

Find the amount after one year at nominal 12% annual interest compounded half-yearly on ₹1,000.

**Answer:** ₹1,123.60, using 1,000 × 1.06².

# GATE 2027 countdown — checked 18 November 2026

The [official IIT Madras important dates](https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in/important_dates) list GATE 2027 examinations on **6, 7, 13, 14, 20 and 21 February 2027**. The [official notifications](https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in/notifications) and main calendar were also checked.

The app now counts down to **6 February 2027**, explicitly labelled the beginning of the official exam window. All six dates are displayed. The previous blank schedule incorrectly presented the whole GATE exam as unannounced.

A CS-specific paper date/session was not established in the official sources checked. It remains separate and unset; 6 February must not be described as the confirmed CS exam date. Once verified, that slot can replace the window countdown. Personal practice targets remain independent of the official schedule.

The Home page selects the next announced exam day from the available schedules instead of always showing Group 2. Existing GATE marking-rule versions and saved test sessions are preserved because this change only updates calendar metadata.

# Paper quality review

1. Open the applicable official notification and a released paper or official sample. Record the cycle, stage, syllabus, question types, section allocations, time, total marks, and penalties. Label models, reconstructions, and partial question pools according to their provenance.
2. Plan difficulty by section. Mix short factual or procedural checks with applications and multi-step problems. Set the mix using released items where available. For each harder item identify the decision or transformation that makes it harder. A long stem or a larger number is insufficient evidence of difficulty.
3. Draft answer choices from plausible errors, such as reversing a conditional, using the wrong denominator, reading the wrong series, or carrying a constraint into a case where it no longer applies. Keep the choices comparable in precision and scope. Preserve standard choice formats when the exam calls for them.
4. Solve each question again from the published prompt and data before reading its stored key. Check every alternative for ambiguity and every calculation against the chart, table, passage, or official source. Where a puzzle or generated calculation permits enumeration, verify the result programmatically. Record an explanation that shows the decisive steps.
5. Read adjacent questions and the whole paper in order. Replace repeated stems, reused choices, or questions that test the same process with only a number or name changed. Shared passages and datasets can support distinct tasks, such as inference, evaluation, and applying a result. Compare against the rest of the library as well.
6. Run `npm run audit:papers -- --details` and `npm test`. Resolve every error. Inspect the reported similarity pairs and repeated choice sets, especially the retained SBI source formats. Confirm the generated files reflect their authoring sources and take the paper through the normal test and answer-review screens.

## Official references reviewed for this update

- Station Master CBT follows CEN 08/2026 §15.1. The notice provides the 100-question, 120-minute pattern and the one-third penalty. GateLabs uses the indicative 30/30/20/20 split. A released paper for this standalone notice was unavailable for calibration. The Section Controller familiarisation material is an adjacent reference. [Ministry of Railways notice links](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2329071&lang=1&reg=3), [CEN 08/2026 copy](https://files.govtjobsalert.in/2026/10/rrb-station-master-cen-08-2026-detailed-notification-out-notification-2.pdf), [RRB Section Controller exam material](https://www.rrbcdg.gov.in/2025-04-sctrl.php)
- APPSC Group II uses separate degree-standard papers for Screening, Mains I, and Mains II. Each is 150 questions, 150 minutes, and 150 marks, with a one-third penalty. Use older released papers as a style and complexity reference alongside the current Annexure II. [APPSC notification listing](https://portal-psc.ap.gov.in/), [Notification 37/2026 copy](https://media-abn.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/media/2026/20260822/APPSC_Group_II_Notification_2026_a0227caa12.pdf), [APPSC released papers and final keys](https://portal-psc.ap.gov.in/HomePages/KeysToPapers)
- SBI Junior Associate prelims uses 30 English, 35 Numerical Ability and 35 Reasoning Ability questions, with 20 minutes per section and a one-quarter penalty per incorrect answer. The samples support checking vocabulary, reading, numerical work and deductions within the section limits. [SBI prelims handout and examples](https://sbi.co.in/documents/77530/43947057/070425-SBI%2B%28Ju%2BAssociates%29%2BIH%2BPhase%2BI%2B2025%2BEng.pdf/d509370e-e3d6-f6a6-115f-5b07b34a6c9b?t=1744009427044)
- GATE CS uses MCQ, MSQ and NAT, with 65 questions and 100 marks over 180 minutes. A full mock has 15 General Aptitude, 13 Engineering Mathematics and 72 remaining CS marks. MCQ penalties depend on the marks assigned. MSQ and NAT receive zero negative marks. Use the official paper and key together for historical questions. [GATE 2027 pattern](https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in/question_paper_pattern), [GATE 2026 master papers and keys](https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/QPs-answer-keys.html)

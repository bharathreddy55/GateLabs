# Current-affairs question snapshot — 16 November 2026

The original archival block was replaced with 30 original questions covering selected 2025–2026 events: 10 AP, 10 national and 10 international. The question-authoring source is `scripts/current-affairs-2026.mjs`; each published question retains its own URL and `asOf` date. This is a bounded model-paper selection, not an exhaustive news digest.

Sources consulted:

- [AP budget portal](https://apfinance.gov.in/...Bud@et26-27/) and [official English budget speech PDF](https://apfinance.gov.in/...Bud@et26-27/documents/SpeechEnglish.pdf). The portal’s numerical tables retain older year labels despite the current heading. Question amounts therefore use paragraph 86 of the PDF; programme facts use paragraphs 31–32 and 77.
- [Union Budget 2026–27 highlights, Ministry of Finance/PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2221455&lang=3&reg=3). Questions distinguish announcements and estimates from realised outcomes.
- [ISRO January 2025 summary](https://www.isro.gov.in/media_isro/pdf/Monthly_summary/Montlhly_summary_January2025.pdf), [SpaDeX docking release](https://www.isro.gov.in/spadex_docking.html), [undocking release](https://www.isro.gov.in/ISRO_EN/spadex_undocking_successful.html), and [2025 achievements](https://www.isro.gov.in/ISRO_EN/Achievements_Department_of_Space_2025.html).
- [UNFCCC COP30 proceedings](https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/cp2025_12_adv.pdf). The date question explicitly asks the actual dates recorded in the proceedings, including the closing day, rather than the initial advertised schedule.
- [G20 2025 leaders’ declaration](https://g20.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2025-G20-Summit-Declaration.pdf), corroborated by the [European Council summit record](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/meetings/international-summit/2025/11/22-23/).

Nobel 2026 pages returned 403 during verification; those proposed questions were excluded. Continue adding dated AP, national and international material as the exam approaches. The official syllabus does not establish a fixed current-affairs lookback period.
